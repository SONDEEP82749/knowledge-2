Frequently Asked Questions

This FAQ answers common questions about Calculator Library Portal, calculator results, privacy, technical operation, advertising and website usage.

General Questions

What is Calculator Library Portal?

Calculator Library Portal is a collection of online calculators covering different practical and educational calculation requirements.

Are the calculators free?

The calculators are generally intended to be available free of charge. Availability of individual tools may change as the website develops.

Do I need to create an account?

The basic calculator library does not generally require an account simply to use a calculator.

Calculator Results

Are calculator results guaranteed to be accurate?

No calculation tool should be treated as an absolute guarantee. Results may depend on formulas, assumptions, rounding and input values. Important financial, medical, legal or professional decisions should be independently verified.

Why is my result different from another calculator?

Different calculators may use different formulas, assumptions, rounding methods, compounding periods, units or definitions. Check the methodology and inputs used by both tools.

What should I do if I find an incorrect result?

Contact us through the Support page or Contact page. Include the calculator name, input values and expected result if possible.

Privacy

Do you collect my calculator inputs?

Many calculator functions can operate directly within the browser. However, the exact handling can vary by tool and technical implementation. Please review the Privacy Policy.

Should I enter sensitive information?

No. Avoid entering passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers or confidential information into public calculator tools unless a specific service explicitly requires and securely supports it.

Cookies and Advertising

Does the website use cookies?

The website and third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies. See our Cookies Policy.

Why do I see advertisements?

Advertisements may be displayed to support the operation and development of the website. Third-party advertising providers may use their own technologies and policies.

Technical Questions

Why is a calculator not working?

Try refreshing the page, checking your inputs, disabling conflicting browser extensions and using a current browser. If the problem continues, contact Support.

Does the website work on mobile?

The website is designed to be responsive and usable on modern smartphones, tablets and desktop browsers.

Professional Use

Can I use calculator results for financial decisions?

Calculators can help with estimates and education but should not replace professional financial advice or official calculations.

Can I use the calculators for medical decisions?

Medical-related calculators are informational only and should not replace qualified medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
