  • Seek research assistance: Librarians are available to provide guidance on research methods, database searching, and citation management.
  • Take advantage of study spaces: The libraries offer a variety of study spaces, including quiet areas, group study rooms, and collaborative workspaces.
  • Participate in library workshops: The libraries offer workshops on topics such as information literacy, citation management, and research methods.
  • Explore digital collections: The libraries provide access to a range of digital collections, including e-books, online journals, and streaming media.
  • Use library technology: The libraries offer a range of technology, including laptops, tablets, and software, to support your academic work.
  • Join a study group: The libraries often host study groups and peer-led team learning sessions, which can be a great way to connect with classmates and stay motivated.
  • Stay up-to-date with library news: Follow the libraries on social media or subscribe to their newsletter to stay informed about new resources, services, and events.
  • Provide feedback: The libraries value your feedback and use it to improve their services and resources.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

While using the resources available at Montclair State University Libraries, it's essential to avoid common mistakes that can hinder your academic progress. These include:
