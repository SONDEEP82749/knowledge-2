10 Actionable Strategies to Succeed at Montclair State University Libraries

Strategy 1: Master the Library Portal & Catalog

The Montclair State University Libraries website is your first stop. The library catalog (powered by WorldCat Discovery) allows you to search for books, journals, and digital media in one place.

How to Use It Effectively:

  • Use advanced search filters (e.g., date range, format, subject).
  • Try Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) for precise searches.
  • Save frequently used searches with alerts for new additions.

Real-World Example: A history major researching the American Civil War might search: "Civil War" AND "soldier experience" NOT "battlefield tactics" to narrow results to personal narratives.

Common Mistake: Relying only on Google Scholar—library databases often provide full-text access where Google Scholar doesn’t.

Strategy 2: Leverage Digital Collections & E-Books

Montclair State Libraries offer thousands of e-books through platforms like EBSCOhost, ProQuest, and JSTOR. These are instantly accessible from anywhere with an internet connection.

How to Access Them:

  1. Go to the Databases A-Z page on the library website.
  2. Select your subject (e.g., "Business," "Psychology").
  3. Use Google Scholar integration to find e-books linked directly from search results.

Real-World Example: A business student working on a marketing thesis can access Harvard Business Review case studies via JSTOR without leaving their dorm room.

Pro Tip: Bookmark subject-specific databases (e.g., PsycINFO for psychology, IEEE Xplore for engineering) to save time.

Strategy 3: Use Interlibrary Loan (ILL) for Unavailable Resources

Not every book or article is in Montclair’s collection—but Interlibrary Loan (ILL) lets you borrow from other institutions.

How to Request:

  1. Search the catalog for the item.
  2. Click "Request via Interlibrary Loan" if unavailable.
  3. Fill out the form (usually takes 3-7 business days).

Real-World Example: A literature student needing a rare 19th-century novel not held by Montclair can request it via ILL and receive a digital scan within a week.

Common Mistake: Forgetting to check your ILL account for updates—set up email notifications to avoid delays.

Strategy 4: Attend Library Workshops & Research Help Sessions

Montclair State Libraries offer free workshops on:

  • Citation management (Zotero, EndNote)
  • Data analysis (SPSS, R)
  • Academic writing & critical thinking

How to Find Workshops:

  • Check the Library Events Calendar.
  • Sign up via Montclair’s Canvas portal.

Real-World Example: A sociology graduate student attended a SPSS workshop and improved their statistical analysis skills, leading to a stronger thesis.

Pro Tip: Even if you’re an expert, attending workshops reinforces best practices and introduces new tools.

Strategy 5: Utilize Research Assistants & Librarians

Librarians at Montclair State are not just reference staff—they’re research partners. They can help with:

  • Narrowing down topics
  • Finding primary sources
  • Evaluating sources for credibility

How to Get Help:

  • Chat with a Librarian (available 24/7 via the website).
  • Schedule a research appointment (in-person or virtual).

Real-World Example: A political science student struggling with finding credible polls on a topic was guided by a librarian to CQ Researcher and Pew Research Center databases, saving hours of searching.

Common Mistake: Waiting until the last minute to ask for help—librarians can save you weeks of frustration.

Strategy 6: Optimize Your Study Spaces

Montclair Libraries offer multiple study environments:

  • Silent Study Zones (for focused work)
  • Group Study Rooms (reservable online)
  • Tech Lounge (with laptops, scanners, and printers)

How to Choose the Right Space:

  • Exams? Use the silent floor (3rd floor, Library North).
  • Group project? Reserve a study room via the Montclair Study Room Booking System.
  • Need a printer? Check the Tech Lounge (Library South) for available machines.

Real-World Example: A business student preparing for final exams used the silent study zone and completed double the work compared to their noisy dorm room.

Pro Tip: Arrive early—popular study rooms fill up fast!

Strategy 7: Use AI & Research Tools for Efficiency

Montclair Libraries integrate AI-driven tools to enhance research:

  • RefWorks (automated citations)
  • Zotero (organizing sources)
  • AI Summarization Tools (for long articles)

How to Use Them:

  • RefWorks: Drag-and-drop PDFs, auto-generate citations.
  • Zotero: Tag and annotate sources for easy retrieval.
  • AI Summarizers: Use Jasper or QuillBot to extract key points from research papers.

Real-World Example: A computer science student used Zotero to organize 50+ papers for their AI ethics paper, making their bibliography error-free and well-structured.

Common Mistake: Not backing up your RefWorks/Zotero library—always sync with cloud storage.

Strategy 8: Explore Special Collections & Archives

Montclair’s Special Collections hold rare books, manuscripts, and historical documents, including:

  • Civil Rights Movement archives
  • Local New Jersey history
  • Literary manuscripts

How to Access:

  • Request appointments via the Special Collections website.
  • Search the Montclair Digital Collections for digitized materials.

Real-World Example: A history professor used Montclair’s Civil Rights Archive to find primary sources for a seminar on NJ activism, which became the basis for a published article.

Pro Tip: Special collections are goldmines for unique research—don’t overlook them!

Strategy 9: Take Advantage of 24/7 Access & Remote Services

Montclair Libraries offer round-the-clock access to:

  • Digital databases
  • E-books
  • Online research help

How to Use Remote Services:

  • 24/7 Chat Reference (available via the website).
  • Digital Scanning (submit requests for physical books to be scanned).
  • Off-Campus VPN Access (for secure database logins).

Real-World Example: A nursing student working a night shift accessed PubMed and CINAHL via the 24/7 database portal to complete a late-night assignment.

Common Mistake: Not setting up VPN before off-campus access—always bookmark the VPN login page.

Strategy 10: Stay Updated with Library News & Policies

Libraries frequently update policies, services, and resources. Stay informed by:

  • Following @MontclairLibraries on social media.
  • Subscribing to the Library Newsletter.
  • Checking the "What’s New" section on the library website.

Real-World Example: A student unaware of new database subscriptions missed out on exclusive access to the New York Times Archive—always check for updates!

Pro Tip: Bookmark the library’s "Policies & FAQ" page for quick reference.

Common Mistakes New Students Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Mistake 1: Not Using Subject-Specific Databases

Problem: Many students rely on Google Scholar or Wikipedia instead of library databases. Solution: Always start with subject-specific databases (e.g., JSTOR for humanities, IEEE Xplore for engineering).

Mistake 2: Ignoring Librarian Help

Problem: Students wait until the last minute to ask for research assistance. Solution: Schedule a research appointment early—librarians can save you months of work.

Mistake 3: Overlooking Digital Collections

Problem: Some students only use physical books, missing e-books and digitized archives. Solution: Explore the "Digital Collections" section—many rare materials are available online.

Mistake 4: Not Setting Up Alerts for New Resources

Problem: Students miss new books, journal articles, or database updates. Solution: Enable email alerts for your search terms in WorldCat and databases.

Mistake 5: Forgetting to Renew Borrowed Items

Problem: Late fees can hurt your academic record. Solution: Check due dates and renew early via your library account.

FAQ Section: Your Montclair State University Libraries Questions Answered

❓ How do I access e-books and journals from off-campus?

✅ Answer: You’ll need to log in via Montclair’s VPN (available on the library website). Once logged in, you can access all e-books and databases without restrictions.

❓ Can I request books from other universities through Interlibrary Loan (ILL)?

✅ Answer: Yes! If Montclair doesn’t have the book, you can submit an ILL request through the library catalog. Most requests arrive within 3-7 business days.

❓ Are there quiet study areas available in the libraries?

✅ Answer: Absolutely! Library North (3rd floor) has a silent study zone perfect for exams and focused work. Library South also offers low-noise areas for individual study.

❓ How do I schedule a group study room?

✅ Answer: You can reserve a group study room via the Montclair Study Room Booking System (available on the library website). Rooms are available for 2-4 hours and can be booked up to two weeks in advance.

❓ What should I do if I can’t find a book in the library?

✅ Answer:

  1. Check the catalog again for possible typos.
  2. Search WorldCat to see if it’s available elsewhere.
  3. Request via Interlibrary Loan if not held by Montclair.
  4. Contact a librarian for help narrowing your search.
