Chapter 1: Getting Acquainted with Montclair State University Libraries

Before we jump into strategies, it’s crucial to understand the structure and offerings of Montclair State’s library system. The university operates three main libraries, each with unique strengths:

1. The Library at the Center for Academic & Creative Excellence (CACE)

  • Location: Main campus (inside the CACE building)
  • Key Features:
    • 24/7 study spaces, including group collaboration rooms and quiet zones
    • High-tech research stations with 3D printing, scanning, and digital humanities tools
    • Special collections, including rare books, manuscripts, and historical documents
    • **Access to ProQuest, JSTOR, and EBSCOhost—essential for academic research

2. The Library at the School of Business

  • Location: School of Business building
  • Key Features:
    • Specialized business databases (e.g., IBISWorld, Mergent Online, Factiva)
    • Case study collections for management and economics research
    • Career resources, including resume workshops and LinkedIn guidance

3. The Library at the School of Education

  • Location: School of Education building
  • Key Features:
    • Education-specific databases (e.g., ERIC, Education Full Text)
    • Curriculum development tools and teacher resource guides
    • **Access to NAEYC (National Association for the Education of Young Children) journals

Digital & Online Resources

  • Montclair State University Libraries’ Website: https://www.montclair.edu/library
  • LibGuides: Subject-specific research guides (e.g., Psychology, Engineering, History)
  • Interlibrary Loan (ILL): Borrow books from other NJ libraries in 3-5 business days
  • Open Access Initiatives: Free, peer-reviewed articles via Montclair Scholars

Chapter 2: 8 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State University Libraries

Now that you know what’s available, let’s explore how to use it effectively. These strategies will save you time, improve your grades, and enhance your research skills.

Strategy 1: Leverage the Librarian Research Consultations (Free & Highly Effective)

Many students don’t know that Montclair State offers one-on-one research help from librarians who specialize in your field.

How to Book a Session:

  1. Visit Montclair State Library Research Help
  2. Select your subject (e.g., Psychology, Engineering, Education)
  3. Schedule a 30-60 minute appointment via email or Zoom

Real-World Example: A senior psychology major, Alex, was struggling to find peer-reviewed studies on adolescent depression. During his research consultation, his librarian guided him to PsycINFO and taught him Boolean search operators (AND, OR, NOT) to refine his results. Within two hours, Alex had a stronger literature review for his thesis.

Pro Tip:

  • Ask for help early—don’t wait until the last minute!
  • Bring your topic, keywords, and any initial sources you’ve found.

Strategy 4: Utilize the Interlibrary Loan (ILL) Service for Books Not in Montclair’s Collection

If Montclair doesn’t have the book or journal article you need, Interlibrary Loan (ILL) can get it for you in 3-5 days.

How to Request an ILL:

  1. Go to Montclair ILL Page
  2. Fill out the request form (include title, author, and your Montclair email)
  3. Wait 3-5 business days for delivery (digital or physical)

Real-World Example: *A graduate student in English Literature needed a rare 19th-century novel not held by Montclair. Through ILL, she received a digital scan within 48 hours, allowing her to complete her analysis on time.

Common Mistake: ❌ Not checking if Montclair already owns the book—first search the library catalog before requesting ILL.

How to Use Zotero:

  1. Download Zotero from Zotero.org
  2. Create a free account (syncs across devices)
  3. Save PDFs, bookmarks, and citations automatically
  4. Generate citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.

Real-World Example: *A sociology major used Zotero to:

  • Collect 50+ sources for her senior thesis
  • Auto-generate citations in APA format
  • Avoid last-minute formatting errors

This **saved her 10+ hours of manual work.

Common Mistake: ❌ Not backing up citations—always sync with Zotero Cloud or export to Google Drive.

Chapter 3: Common Mistakes New Students Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Even the most motivated students can fall into time-wasting traps when using the library. Here’s what to watch out for:

Mistake #1: Relying Only on Google Scholar (Instead of Montclair’s Databases)

❌ Problem: Google Scholar doesn’t always provide full-text access to paid journals. ✅ Solution: Use Montclair’s databases first (JSTOR, ProQuest, EBSCOhost) for full-text, peer-reviewed articles.

Mistake #2: Not Using Librarian Research Help

❌ Problem: Many students don’t know that librarians can help refine searches. ✅ Solution: Book a research consultation—it’s free and worth it.

Mistake #3: Ignoring Interlibrary Loan (ILL) Deadlines

❌ Problem: If you wait until the last day to request an ILL, you might miss deadlines. ✅ Solution: Request ILLs at least 5 days before you need them.

Mistake #4: Not Checking for Full-Text Availability

❌ Problem: Finding a great article but not being able to read it due to paywall restrictions. ✅ Solution: Click "Check for Full Text" in Montclair’s databases—it will link you to legal, free copies.

Mistake #5: Overlooking Special Collections for Unique Research

❌ Problem: Missing out on exclusive primary sources that no other library has. ✅ Solution: Explore Special Collections if you’re doing advanced research.

Mistake #6: Not Using Study Spaces Strategically

❌ Problem: Cramming in noisy areas when you need focus. ✅ Solution: Choose the right study space (e.g., quiet zones for exams, group rooms for projects).

Mistake #7: Forgetting to Renew Books Before Due Dates

❌ Problem: Late fees can add up quickly. ✅ Solution: Renew books online via Montclair Library Catalog.

Mistake #8: Not Attending Library Workshops

❌ Problem: Missing out on free skill-building (e.g., data analysis, citation tools). ✅ Solution: Check the Library Events Calendar and register early.
