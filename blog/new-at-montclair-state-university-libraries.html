Step 1: Getting Started – Setting Up Your Library Account & Accessing Resources

Before you can fully utilize Montclair State University Libraries, you need to activate your library account and understand how to browse, borrow, and return materials seamlessly.

How to Create & Activate Your Library Account

  1. Log in via the Library Portal

    • Go to Montclair State University Libraries Website.
    • Click "Library Catalog" (or "Search the Library").
    • Enter your Montclair State username and password (same as your MyMontclair credentials).

  2. Verify Your Account Details

    • Ensure your name, email, and borrowing privileges are correct.
    • If you see any errors (e.g., incorrect email), contact the Circulation Desk at 973-655-7560 or library@montclair.edu.

  3. Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for Security

    • Some databases (like ProQuest or JSTOR) require 2FA for off-campus access.
    • Use Google Authenticator or Duo Mobile to secure your account.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Many students forget to check their Montclair email for library notifications (e.g., overdue fines, new database access). ✅ Fix: Enable email alerts in your library account settings.

Step 2: Navigating the Library Catalog – Finding Books & Digital Resources

Montclair State Libraries use WorldCat Discovery (a powerful search tool) to help you locate books, e-books, journals, and more. Here’s how to search efficiently:

Pro Tips for Advanced Searching

  1. Use Boolean Operators (AND, OR, NOT)

    • Example: "climate change AND policy NOT fiction"
    • This narrows results to only academic sources on climate policy.

  2. Filter by Format & Availability

    • After searching, use the left-hand filters to:
      • Limit to "Available Online" (for e-books)
      • Filter by "Publication Date" (e.g., last 5 years)
      • Sort by "Relevance" or "Date Added" (for the latest research)

  3. Check the "Full Text" Checkbox

    • Many databases only show abstracts unless you select "Full Text" to see complete articles.

  4. Use the "Citation Search" Feature

    • If you have a known citation, paste it into the search bar to find the exact source.

🔍 Real-World Example: A political science student needs books on voting behavior in New Jersey.

  • They search: "voting behavior AND New Jersey AND 2020-2024"
  • They filter for "Books" and "Available Online", finding 3 relevant e-books in minutes.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Students search too broadly (e.g., just "psychology") and get overwhelmed by irrelevant results. ✅ Fix: Be specific—use keywords from your syllabus or research question.

Step 3: Borrowing Books & Managing Your Account

Montclair State Libraries offer multiple borrowing options, including physical books, course reserves, and interlibrary loan (ILL).

Borrowing Physical Books

  • Loan Periods:
    • Undergraduates: 28 days (renewable once if no holds)
    • Graduates/Faculty: 56 days (renewable twice)
    • Course Reserves: 2-hour or overnight loans (check the shelf for details)
  • Fines & Overdue Policies:
    • $0.25 per day after the due date.
    • Holds will prevent you from checking out more books.

📌 How to Renew Books:

  • Online: Log in to your account and click "Renew" under the book title.
  • In-Person: Visit the Circulation Desk with your student ID.

Interlibrary Loan (ILL) – Borrowing from Other Libraries

If Montclair doesn’t have a book you need:

  1. Search the catalog and click "Request via Interlibrary Loan."
  2. Fill out the form (takes 24-72 hours).
  3. Pick up at the Circulation Desk (some books arrive digitally).

⚠️ Common Mistake: Students forget to check their Montclair email for ILL notifications. ✅ Fix: Set up email alerts in your ILL account.

Step 5: Using Research Tools – Citation Managers & Plagiarism Checkers

Managing citations and avoiding plagiarism are critical skills for academic success. Here’s how to master them:

A. Citation Managers (EndNote, Zotero, Mendeley)

These tools automate citations and organize your research.

1. Zotero (Free & User-Friendly)

  • How to Set Up:
    1. Download from Zotero.org.
    2. Install the browser extension (Chrome/Firefox).
    3. Save PDFs and citations as you research.
  • Pro Tip: Use Zotero’s "Related Items" feature to find additional sources on your topic.

2. EndNote (Paid, but Powerful for Large Projects)

  • Best for: Graduate students, thesis writers.
  • How to Use:
    • Import citations from databases (JSTOR, ProQuest).
    • Auto-generate bibliographies in APA, MLA, Chicago formats.

📌 Real-World Example: *A graduate student in education uses Zotero to:

  • Save 50+ articles on special education policies.
  • Auto-generate an APA bibliography in Word with one click.
  • Share a private library with their research partner.*

B. Plagiarism Checkers (Turnitin, Grammarly)

  • Turnitin (used in many Montclair courses) checks for unoriginal content.
    • How to Use:
      • Submit papers via Blackboard or Montclair’s Turnitin portal.
      • Review the similarity report and cite sources properly.
  • Grammarly (free version available) helps with grammar and paraphrasing.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Students copy-paste from Wikipedia or paraphrase poorly, leading to plagiarism flags. ✅ Fix: Use quotation marks for direct quotes and cite every source (even paraphrased ideas).
