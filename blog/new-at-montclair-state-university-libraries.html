Chapter 1: Getting Started – Understanding Montclair State University Libraries

Before you can use the libraries effectively, you need to familiarize yourself with their structure, services, and key locations.

1.1 The Main Libraries: Where to Find What You Need

Montclair State University operates three main libraries, each serving different academic needs:

1.1.1 The Library at the Center for Academic Success (CAS Library)

  • Location: Center for Academic Success (CAS), 1 Normal Avenue, Montclair, NJ
  • Focus: General academic support, including study spaces, tutoring, and access to popular textbooks.
  • Unique Features:
    • Group study rooms (reservable via the library’s online portal).
    • A quiet study floor for focused work.
    • Access to calculators, laptops, and printing services (with student ID).
    • Workshops on research skills, citation tools, and academic writing.

1.1.2 The Library at the School of Business (SB Library)

  • Location: School of Business Building, 1 Normal Avenue, Montclair, NJ
  • Focus: Business, economics, finance, and management research.
  • Unique Features:
    • Specialized databases like IBISWorld, Mergent Online, and Statista for market research.
    • Case study collections for business strategy courses.
    • Close proximity to the business school, making it ideal for MBA and undergraduate business students.

1.1.3 The Library at the School of Arts & Sciences (SAS Library)

  • Location: School of Arts & Sciences Building, 1 Normal Avenue, Montclair, NJ
  • Focus: Humanities, social sciences, STEM, and interdisciplinary research.
  • Unique Features:
    • Archives & Special Collections (rare books, historical documents, and digital archives).
    • Advanced research tools like JSTOR, Project MUSE, and ScienceDirect.
    • Specialized research guides for disciplines such as psychology, sociology, and computer science.

Pro Tip: Use the Montclair State Libraries’ website to locate each library on campus and check their hours. Some branches have extended hours during finals week!

Chapter 2: 10 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State University Libraries

Now that you know where everything is, let’s explore practical strategies to make the most of your library experience.

Strategy 1: Register for a Library Card & Access Your Account

Before you can borrow books or access digital resources, you need a library card.

  • How to Get One:
    • New students: Your library card is automatically created when you register for classes. Check your Montclair State email for activation instructions.
    • Returning students: Log in to your library account via Montclair State Libraries Online using your NetID and password.
  • Why It Matters:
    • Borrow physical books (with loan periods ranging from 3 days to 4 weeks).
    • Access e-books, journals, and databases from anywhere.
    • Renew books online to avoid late fees.

Real-World Example: Imagine you’re working on a history paper and need a rare 19th-century document. Without a library card, you’d have to rely on expensive online purchases—but with one, you can borrow it for free from the Archives & Special Collections.

Strategy 2: Use the Library’s Search Tool Like a Pro

The Montclair State Libraries Search (available on the library website) is your one-stop shop for finding books, articles, and more. Here’s how to use it effectively:

  1. Start with a broad search (e.g., "climate change in New Jersey").
  2. Refine using filters (publication date, subject, format).
  3. Check the "Full Text" filter to ensure you’re accessing free, readable articles.
  4. Use advanced search for Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) to narrow results.

Pro Tip: If you’re struggling to find what you need, try different keywords or ask a librarian for help via chat or email.

Strategy 3: Leverage Digital Databases for Research

Montclair’s libraries offer thousands of databases, but not all are equally useful. Here’s how to pick the right ones:

Database Best For How to Access
JSTOR Humanities, social sciences, STEM Link (via library website)
ScienceDirect Scientific research papers Link
ProQuest News articles, dissertations Link
Google Scholar Free academic papers Link (set Montclair as your library)

Real-World Example: A psychology student researching cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) could use PsychINFO (via ProQuest) to find peer-reviewed studies—something you can’t get for free on Google.

Strategy 4: Borrow Books & Manage Your Loans Smartly

Montclair’s libraries allow you to borrow up to 50 items at once, but late fees add up fast. Here’s how to stay organized:

  • Set reminders for due dates (use email alerts from your library account).
  • Renew books online if you need more time.
  • Return books early to avoid fines (especially during finals week).
  • Use the "Hold" feature if a book is checked out—you’ll get an email when it’s available.

Common Mistake: Assuming all books are available in print. Many are only in digital format—always check the "Availability" tab.

Strategy 5: Use the Library’s Study Spaces & Tech Tools

Montclair’s libraries offer more than just books—they provide study spaces, tech tools, and quiet zones.

  • Group Study Rooms (reservable via the library website).
  • Individual Study Carrels (first-come, first-served).
  • Laptops & Calculators (available for checkout with your ID).
  • 3D Printing & Scanning Services (for STEM and design projects).

Pro Tip: If you’re working on a group project, book a study room in advance—they fill up fast during exam periods!

Strategy 6: Attend Library Workshops & Research Help Sessions

Montclair’s libraries host free workshops on:

  • Citation tools (Zotero, EndNote)
  • Academic writing & plagiarism avoidance
  • Data analysis (SPSS, R, Python)
  • Thesis & dissertation research

How to Find Workshops:

  • Check the Library Events Calendar.
  • Sign up via Montclair’s student portal.

Real-World Example: *A grad student struggling with SPSS for statistics attended a workshop and saved hours of trial-and-error, improving their research efficiency by 30%.

Strategy 7: Access E-Books & Audiobooks for On-the-Go Learning

Montclair’s e-book collection includes thousands of titles, including:

  • Sage Research Methods (for research design).
  • O’Reilly for Public Safety (for criminal justice students).
  • Audible & Libby (for audiobooks).

How to Access:

  • Use the "E-Books" filter in the library search.
  • Download the Libby app for mobile access.

Common Mistake: Assuming e-books are only for leisure—they’re perfect for late-night study sessions when you can’t visit the library.

Strategy 8: Use the Archives & Special Collections for Unique Research

Montclair’s Archives & Special Collections hold rare materials that can’t be found anywhere else, including:

  • Historical documents from the 1800s.
  • Local NJ newspapers (dating back to the 19th century).
  • Digital archives of Montclair State’s history.

How to Access:

  • Request materials via the Archives Research Guide.
  • Schedule an appointment with an archivist for deep research.

Real-World Example: *A history student researching Montclair’s role in the Civil Rights Movement found primary sources in the archives that directly influenced their thesis.

Strategy 9: Print, Scan, & Save Documents Efficiently

Montclair’s libraries offer free printing, scanning, and digital saving—but there are rules to follow:

  • Printing Costs: ~$0.10 per black & white page.
  • Scanning: Use library computers or your phone (with Montclair’s Wi-Fi).
  • Saving to Cloud: Many computers have Google Drive & OneDrive pre-installed.

Pro Tip: If you’re printing a 50-page paper, consider saving it as a PDF and emailing it to yourself instead.

Strategy 10: Ask for Help When You Need It

Montclair’s librarians are experts in research and can save you hours of frustration. Ways to get help:

  • Live Chat (available on the library website).
  • Email Reference (libref@montclair.edu).
  • In-Person Help (at the Research Help Desk).
  • Scheduled Appointments (for complex research projects).

Real-World Example: *A business student struggling with financial data analysis met with a librarian who recommended the right database and taught them how to filter results—resulting in a stronger final paper.

Chapter 3: Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Even the best students make mistakes when using libraries. Here’s how to avoid the most common pitfalls:

Mistake 1: Not Checking Library Hours Before Visiting

  • Problem: Walking into a closed library because you assumed it was open.
  • Solution: Always check the Library Hours Page before going.

Mistake 2: Ignoring E-Books & Only Using Print Books

  • Problem: Missing out on 24/7 access to thousands of books.
  • Solution: Search for e-books first—they’re often faster and more convenient.

Mistake 3: Not Using the "Hold" Feature

  • Problem: A book you need is checked out, and you don’t know when it will be available.
  • Solution: Place a hold—you’ll get an email when it’s ready.

Mistake 4: Forgetting to Renew Books

  • Problem: Late fees add up, hurting your student budget.
  • Solution: Set email reminders or renew online before the due date.

Mistake 5: Not Attending Workshops

  • Problem: Missing out on free training that could improve your research skills.
  • Solution: Check the workshop calendar and sign up early.

Mistake 6: Overlooking Special Collections

  • Problem: Missing unique, primary sources that enhance your research.
  • Solution: Explore the Archives & Special Collections for exclusive materials.

Mistake 7: Printing Too Much (Wasting Money & Paper)

  • Problem: Unexpected printing costs can drain your budget.
  • Solution: Print only what you need or save as a PDF.

1. How do I find a specific book in Montclair’s libraries?

Answer: To find a book, use the Montclair State Libraries Search:

  1. Type the title, author, or subject in the search bar.
  2. If the book is available, note the location (CAS, SB, or SAS Library) and call number.
  3. Use the "Get It" button to check out, request, or place a hold.
  4. If the book is checked out, use the "Hold" option to reserve it.
