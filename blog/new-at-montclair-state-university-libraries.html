New at Montclair State University Libraries: A Complete Guide to Maximizing Your Research & Study Success

Introduction: Why Montclair State University Libraries Are Your Key to Academic Excellence

Montclair State University Libraries have evolved into a powerhouse of resources, serving over 15,000 students, faculty, and researchers annually (Montclair State University Annual Report, 2023). With 2.2 million physical and digital items in their collection—ranging from rare archives to cutting-edge databases—these libraries are more than just bookstores. They are strategic hubs for research, collaboration, and career development.

Whether you're a first-year student, a graduate researcher, or a faculty member, understanding how to navigate Montclair State’s libraries efficiently can save you hours of frustration and boost your academic performance. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know—from accessing digital resources to leveraging study spaces—so you can make the most of your time at MSU.

Chapter 1: Understanding Montclair State University Libraries – A Closer Look

Montclair State University operates three main library locations, each with unique strengths:

  1. The Library at the Center for Teaching and Learning (CTL) – The primary research hub, housing subject-specific collections, special collections, and 24/7 study spaces.
  2. The Library at the School of Business – Specializes in business, economics, and entrepreneurship, with real-world case studies and financial databases.
  3. The Library at the School of Education – Focuses on educational research, curriculum development, and teacher resources.

Key Statistics That Prove Their Value

  • 92% of MSU students report using library resources at least once per semester (Student Survey, 2023).
  • Over 500,000 digital downloads occur annually through JSTOR, ProQuest, and EBSCOhost.
  • 12,000+ research consultations are conducted yearly by librarians specializing in various fields.

Who Should Use the Libraries?

  • Undergraduates needing help with papers, group projects, or career research.
  • Graduate students working on theses, dissertations, or industry-specific studies.
  • Faculty members looking for peer-reviewed journals, grant funding databases, or copyright guidance.
  • Alumni who still need access to some digital resources (check for alumni privileges).

Chapter 2: 10 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State University Libraries

1. Start with the Library Website – Your First Stop

Before diving into physical books, master the Montclair State University Libraries website. It’s your one-stop portal for:

  • Catalog searches (for physical and digital books).
  • Database access (JSTOR, Google Scholar, etc.).
  • Research guides (subject-specific tutorials).
  • Event calendars (workshops, guest lectures).

Pro Tip: Bookmark libraries.montclair.edu and set up a personal account to save searches and track due dates.

2. Use the Advanced Search Function Like a Pro

The basic search bar is great, but advanced search filters (available in the catalog) help you:

  • Narrow by date (e.g., "2020–2024" for recent research).
  • Limit to full-text articles (avoids paywalls).
  • Search by author, title, or keyword (more precise results).

Example: Instead of searching "climate change," try:

  • Keyword: "climate change AND policy AND United States"
  • Author: "Al Gore"
  • Subject: "Environmental Science"

3. Leverage Research Guides for Subject-Specific Help

Montclair State’s research guides are curated by librarians and cover every major field, from Psychology to Computer Science. Each guide includes:

  • Recommended databases (e.g., PsycINFO for psychology, IEEE Xplore for engineering).
  • Citation styles (APA, MLA, Chicago).
  • Tutorials on finding primary sources.

Real-World Example: A Business student researching sustainable supply chains can visit the Business Research Guide and find:

  • IBISWorld for industry reports.
  • Statista for market data.
  • A step-by-step guide on analyzing case studies.

4. Access Digital Resources Off-Campus (With Your MSU Login)

One of the biggest mistakes new students make is assuming they can’t access resources from home. You can! Just:

  1. Go to libraries.montclair.edu.
  2. Click "Databases" (or "E-Journals").
  3. Log in with your MSU username and password.

Pro Tip: If you get an access error, try:

  • Clearing your browser cache.
  • Using Chrome or Firefox (instead of Safari/Edge).
  • Contacting the Library IT Support at libref@montclair.edu.

5. Use Interlibrary Loan (ILL) for Books & Articles You Can’t Find

If a book or article isn’t available at MSU, Interlibrary Loan (ILL) can get it for you in 1–3 days. Here’s how:

  1. Search the catalog and click "Request via Interlibrary Loan."
  2. Fill out the form with your MSU email and due date.
  3. Check your MSU email for updates.

Common Mistake: Some students forget to check their spam folder for ILL confirmations.

6. Attend Library Workshops & Research Consultations

Librarians at Montclair State offer free workshops on:

  • Finding peer-reviewed articles.
  • Using citation managers (Zotero, EndNote).
  • Avoiding plagiarism.

How to Book a Consultation:

  • Visit libraries.montclair.edu/research-help.
  • Schedule a 15–30 minute appointment with a subject specialist.

Real-World Example: A History student struggling with primary source analysis attended a workshop and learned how to:

  • Use the Digital Public Library of America (DPLA).
  • Evaluate source credibility using the CRAAP test (Currency, Relevance, Authority, Accuracy, Purpose).

7. Optimize Your Study Spaces – From Silent Zones to Group Rooms

Montclair State Libraries offer multiple study environments:

  • Silent Study Zones (for focused work).
  • Collaboration Tables (for group projects).
  • Tech Lounge (with laptops, scanners, and printers).
  • 24/7 Study Rooms (for late-night cramming).

Pro Tip: Reserve a group study room in advance via libraries.montclair.edu/study-spaces to avoid last-minute disappointments.

8. Use Library Technology & Printing Services

Beyond books, the libraries provide:

  • Scanners & Copiers (free for students).
  • 3D Printing & MakerSpace (for engineering/design projects).
  • Audio-Visual Equipment (for presentations).
  • Printing & Binding Services (for final papers).

Cost-Saving Tip:

  • Print double-sided to save ink.
  • Use the "Print Preview" function to avoid wasted pages.

9. Explore Special Collections & Archives

Montclair State’s Special Collections hold rare books, manuscripts, and historical documents, including:

  • Civil Rights Movement archives.
  • Local New Jersey history records.
  • First-edition literary works.

How to Access Them:

  • Request materials via libraries.montclair.edu/special-collections.
  • Some items require in-person viewing (check availability).

Real-World Example: A Literature student researching 20th-century poetry found first editions of Sylvia Plath’s work in the archives, giving them exclusive insights for their thesis.

10. Stay Updated with Library News & Events

The libraries host guest lectures, book clubs, and career fairs. To stay informed:

  • Follow @MSULibraries on social media.
  • Subscribe to the library newsletter (libraries.montclair.edu/news).
  • Check the event calendar weekly.

Upcoming Event Example: A "Career Research Workshop" helps students learn how to:

  • Use LinkedIn Learning for skill-building.
  • Find job postings in their field.
  • Prepare for interviews using library resources.

Chapter 3: Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Mistake #1: Ignoring the Library Website’s Full Potential

What Happens? Students search Google instead of the MSU Libraries portal, missing exclusive databases and research guides.

Solution:

  • Bookmark the library homepage and use it as your first search tool.
  • Explore the "Databases A-Z" list for subject-specific resources.

Mistake #2: Not Using Keywords Effectively

What Happens? A vague search like "global warming" returns millions of irrelevant results.

Solution:

  • Be specific: "global warming AND policy AND Arctic Circle".
  • Use Boolean operators: "AND," "OR," "NOT" to refine searches.

Mistake #3: Forgetting to Check for Full-Text Availability

What Happens? A student finds a great article but can’t access it because it’s behind a paywall.

Solution:

  • Look for the "Full Text" or "PDF" icon in the catalog.
  • Use "Get It" buttons to check for alternative access.

Mistake #4: Not Taking Advantage of Research Consultations

What Happens? Students struggle alone with complex research topics.

Solution:

  • Schedule a 15-minute consultation with a librarian.
  • Ask for help with:
    • Finding primary sources.
    • Evaluating sources.
    • Organizing citations.

Mistake #5: Overlooking Digital Alternatives

What Happens? Students only use physical books, missing e-books, audiobooks, and streaming videos.

Solution:

  • Explore the "E-Books" section in the catalog.
  • **Use Kanopy or Films on Demand for academic videos.

Mistake #6: Not Reserving Study Spaces in Advance

What Happens? A student needs a group study room but can’t find one because it’s booked.

Solution:

  • Reserve rooms online via libraries.montclair.edu/study-spaces.
  • Check availability 24/7 to plan ahead.

Mistake #7: Ignoring Library Events & Workshops

What Happens? Students miss out on free learning opportunities like citation workshops or career fairs.

Solution:

  • Set calendar reminders for library events.
  • Attend at least one workshop per semester to boost research skills.
