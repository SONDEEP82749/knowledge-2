  • Not Utilizing Library Resources: The libraries provide access to a vast array of resources, including books, journals, and databases. By not taking advantage of these materials, you may be limiting the quality and depth of your research.
  • Not Managing Your Time Effectively: Effective time management is essential for achieving academic success. By not prioritizing your tasks and managing your time wisely, you may struggle to complete assignments and projects on time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What are the library's hours of operation?
The library's hours of operation vary depending on the day and semester, but they are generally open from 8am to 10pm, Monday through Thursday, and 8am to 5pm on Friday.
Q: How do I access the library's online resources?
You can access the library's online resources by visiting the library's website and logging in with your university username and password.
Q: Can I borrow books from other libraries?
Yes, you can borrow books from other libraries through the interlibrary loan service. Simply submit a request through the library's online form, and the library will work to obtain the book for you.
Q: How do I get help with my research project?
