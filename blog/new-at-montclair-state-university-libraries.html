10 Actionable Strategies to Succeed at Montclair State University Libraries

Now that you know what the library offers, let’s break down how to use it effectively.

1. Master Your Library Account (Before You Even Step Inside)

Every Montclair State student gets a library account automatically. Before you need it, log in to your account via the Montclair State University Libraries website to:

  • Check out books (physical and digital)
  • Renew loans (most books have a 28-day loan period)
  • View fines and holds
  • Access your reading list (if assigned by professors)

Pro Tip: Enable email notifications for due dates to avoid late fees.

Real-World Example: Sarah, a freshman in the School of Business, forgot to renew her economics textbook before the deadline. She received an email reminder and renewed it online in time, saving her a $5 late fee.

2. Use the Library’s Search Bar Like a Pro

The library’s main search bar (located on the homepage) is your first tool for finding books, articles, and more. But to get the best results:

✅ Be specific – Instead of searching "climate change," try "climate change in Africa 2020-2023." ✅ Use filters – Narrow results by date, format (e-book, journal article), or subject. ✅ Check the "Availability" tab – See which items are online (instant access) vs. physical (need to visit the library).

Common Mistake: Searching too broadly and getting irrelevant results. Always refine your query.

How to Avoid It:

  • Use Boolean operators (AND, OR, NOT) to narrow searches.
    • Example: "global warming AND policy NOT fiction"

3. Leverage the Library’s Research Guides by Subject

Montclair State Libraries provides pre-made research guides for every major. These are curated collections of the best books, databases, and websites for your field.

How to Find Them:

  1. Go to the Library Research Guides page.
  2. Select your subject (e.g., Psychology, Computer Science, Education).
  3. Browse recommended databases, journals, and key resources.

Real-World Example: James, a grad student in Environmental Science, used the Environmental Studies Research Guide to find peer-reviewed articles on sustainable agriculture—something his professor specifically required.

4. Access Digital Resources Anytime, Anywhere

Montclair State Libraries offers thousands of e-books and online journals. Here’s how to use them:

  • E-books: Available 24/7 via ProQuest Ebook Central, JSTOR, and EBSCOhost.
  • Journals: Search by title or subject in the Journal Finder.
  • Off-Campus Access: Use your Montclair State username and password to log in.

Pro Tip: Bookmark the Library’s Digital Collections page for quick access.

Common Mistake: Not checking if an article is full-text available before downloading.

How to Avoid It:

  • Look for the "PDF" or "Full Text" link in search results.
  • If an article isn’t available, use Interlibrary Loan (ILL) (see Strategy #8).

6. Visit the Library for Physical Resources (When Needed)

While digital resources are convenient, some books are only available physically. Here’s how to make the most of them:

  • Check the catalog before visiting to see which books are in stock.
  • Use the "Find It" button to see if an article is available online or in print.
  • Ask librarians—they can help you locate books on the shelves.

Pro Tip: The Library’s "Reserve" section holds high-demand textbooks for your courses.

Common Mistake: Assuming all books are available online—some are only physical copies.

How to Avoid It:

  • Always check the "Availability" status in the catalog.

8. Master Interlibrary Loan (ILL) for Books Not at Montclair

If Montclair doesn’t have a book you need, Interlibrary Loan (ILL) lets you borrow it from other universities—for free.

How to Request an ILL:

  1. Search the Montclair Library Catalog.
  2. If the book isn’t available, click "Request via Interlibrary Loan."
  3. Fill out the form with your Montclair ID and contact info.
  4. Wait 3-7 business days for delivery (usually via email or pickup at the library).

Pro Tip:

  • Start ILL requests early—some books take up to two weeks.
  • Check your ILL account regularly for updates.

Common Mistake: Not requesting ILL early enough and missing deadlines.

How to Avoid It:

  • Set a reminder 1-2 weeks before a paper is due.

9. Attend Library Workshops & Research Help Sessions

Montclair State Libraries offers free workshops on:

  • Citation & Plagiarism
  • Advanced Database Searching
  • Thesis & Dissertation Writing
  • Data Analysis (for STEM students)

How to Find Workshops:

  • Check the Library Events Calendar.
  • Sign up via Montclair’s Canvas portal or email alerts.

Real-World Example: *A grad student in Computer Science attended a workshop on "Using Python for Data Analysis" and learned how to clean datasets—a skill he used in his capstone project.

10. Ask Librarians for Help (They’re There for You!)

Montclair State Librarians are highly trained research experts who can help with: ✔ Finding sources for your paper ✔ Evaluating websites for credibility ✔ Citing sources (APA, MLA, Chicago) ✔ Navigating databases

How to Get Help:

  • Chat with a Librarian (live chat on the library website).
  • Schedule a Research Appointment (via LibCal).
  • Visit the Research Help Desk (Library East, 1st Floor).

Pro Tip: Librarians can save you hours of wasted searching!

Common Mistake: Assuming you can figure it out alone—librarians are paid to help.

How to Avoid It:

  • Email or chat with a librarian before getting stuck.
