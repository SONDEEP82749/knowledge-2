  • Explore the libraries' digital collections: The Montclair State University Libraries have an extensive collection of digital materials, including e-books, articles, and databases.   • Use the libraries' technology and equipment: The libraries have a range of technology and equipment available for student use, including laptops, tablets, and multimedia software. These resources can be used to create presentations, videos, and other multimedia projects.
  • Participate in the libraries' events and exhibitions: The Montclair State University Libraries host a range of events and exhibitions throughout the year, including author readings, research symposia, and art exhibitions.   • Provide feedback and suggestions: The libraries are committed to providing the best possible services and resources for students. If you have any feedback or suggestions, don't be afraid to share them with library staff.
  • Stay up-to-date with library news and announcements: The libraries regularly post news and announcements on their website and social media channels. Stay informed about new resources, services, and events by following the libraries on social media or signing up for their newsletter.
  • Take care of your physical and mental health: The libraries offer a range of resources and services to support student well-being, including study breaks, mindfulness programs, and access to counseling services. Don't forget to take care of your physical and mental health while you are studying.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

While the Montclair State University Libraries offer a range of resources and services, there are some common mistakes that students can make. These include:

  • Not planning ahead: Failing to plan your research and study time can lead to last-minute cramming and poor academic performance. Make sure to create a schedule and stick to it.
  • Not seeking help when needed: Don't be afraid to ask for help if you are struggling with your research or studies. The libraries' research assistance services and other resources are there to support you.
  • Not evaluating sources critically: It's essential to evaluate your sources critically to ensure that they are reliable and relevant to your research topic. Make sure to consider factors such as author credibility, publication date, and bias.
  • Not managing your time effectively: Poor time management can lead to stress and poor academic performance. Make sure to prioritize your tasks, set realistic goals, and take regular breaks.
