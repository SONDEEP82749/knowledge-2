1.2 Essential Services Every New Student Should Know

Montclair State Libraries offer far more than just books. Here are the must-know services:

  • Library Card & Access

    • All currently enrolled students, faculty, and staff receive a free library card with unlimited borrowing privileges.
    • Graduate students can borrow reference books for 2 weeks (renewable if not requested by others).
    • Undergraduates have 3-week loan periods for most materials.

  • Interlibrary Loan (ILL) – Borrow from Anywhere in the World

    • Need a book MSU doesn’t own? ILL allows you to request it from another institution (usually arrives in 3–7 business days).
    • E-books and articles can be delivered instantly via PDF or digital loan.
    • Cost? Free for students!

  • Research Help Desk & Live Chat

    • In-person assistance at Library North & South (Mon–Thu 8 AM–10 PM, Fri 8 AM–5 PM, Sat 10 AM–5 PM, Sun 1 PM–10 PM).
    • 24/7 Live Chat via the MSU Libraries website for instant answers to citation questions, database troubleshooting, and more.

  • Study & Collaboration Spaces

    • Group study rooms (bookable via the MSU Study Rooms Portal)—great for group projects.
    • Silent study floors (Library North’s 4th floor) for focused work.
    • The Makerspace (Library South) offers 3D printers, laser cutters, and sewing machines—perfect for STEM, art, and design projects.

  • Digital & Print Resources

    • Over 100 databases (JSTOR, ProQuest, EBSCOhost) covering every subject imaginable.
    • Open Access Journals (no paywalls!) via DOAB (Directory of Open Access Books).
    • E-books & Audiobooks via OverDrive, Kanopy (streaming films), and Libby.

Real-World Example: A business student named Priya needed a rare 19th-century economic report for her thesis. Since MSU didn’t have it, she used Interlibrary Loan (ILL) to request it from Princeton University’s library. The PDF arrived in 4 days, and she cited it in her paper using Chicago Style—all with zero cost.

Chapter 2: 10 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State University Libraries

Now that you know the basics, let’s dive into practical strategies to save time, improve research quality, and avoid common mistakes.

Strategy 1: Use the Library’s Advanced Search Tools Like a Pro

Montclair State Libraries’ search bar (found on the MSU Libraries homepage) is powerful—but only if you know how to use it.

How to Search Effectively:

  1. Use Boolean Operators (AND, OR, NOT) to narrow or expand your results.
    • Example: Instead of searching "climate change", try:
      • "climate change" AND "New Jersey" (for localized studies)
      • "climate change" OR "global warming" (to find related terms)
      • "climate change" NOT "politics" (to exclude irrelevant results)
  2. Leverage Filters (after searching):
    • Date Range (e.g., last 5 years for current research).
    • Source Type (peer-reviewed journals, books, news articles).
    • Subject (e.g., "Environmental Policy" for focused results).
  3. Try Subject Guides (e.g., Business Research Guide) for pre-made search paths.

Common Mistake: Searching with too few keywords leads to broad, unhelpful results. Always use 3–5 key terms.

While Google Scholar is convenient, Montclair State’s subscription databases provide more reliable, peer-reviewed, and full-text sources.

Real-World Example: A nursing student, Daniel, needed evidence-based research on diabetes management in elderly patients. Instead of Googling, he used CINAHL and found a 2023 study from Johns Hopkins that directly supported his thesis. Google Scholar would have given him outdated or low-quality sources.

Strategy 3: Master Citation & Avoid Plagiarism with MSU’s Tools

Plagiarism is never an option, but citation mistakes happen more often than you think. Montclair State Libraries provide free tools to help:

  1. Zotero (Free Reference Manager)

    • Automatically generates citations in APA, MLA, Chicago, and more.
    • Syncs across devices (great for group projects).
    • How to start:
      • Download Zotero.
      • Browse databases (e.g., JSTOR) and drag-and-drop sources into your library.
      • Insert citations into Word/Google Docs with one click.

  2. RefWorks (Paid but Worth It for Grad Students)

    • More advanced than Zotero, with AI-powered citation suggestions.
    • Best for: Thesis writers, dissertations, and large-scale research.

  3. MSU’s Citation Guides

    • The MSU Libraries Citation Guide has sample papers in every format (APA, MLA, Chicago, etc.).

Common Mistake: Copy-pasting citations without checking for errors. Always double-check against the Purdue OWL.

Strategy 4: Use the Makerspace for Creative & Technical Projects

The Makerspace (Library South, 2nd floor) is not just for engineers—it’s a hidden gem for:

  • 3D Printing (prototype designs, art installations).
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving (custom posters, jewelry).
  • Sewing & Textile Work (costume design, fashion projects).
  • Audio Recording (music production, podcasts).

How to Access It:

  1. Book a session via the MSU Study Rooms Portal.
  2. Attend a workshop (e.g., "Introduction to 3D Modeling with Tinkercad").
  3. Get trained—staff will guide you on safety and usage.

Real-World Example: A theater major, Mira, needed custom props for her play. She used the Makerspace’s laser cutter to design intricate wooden masks—something she couldn’t buy anywhere else for her budget.

Strategy 5: Leverage Special Collections for Unique Research Opportunities

Montclair State’s Special Collections hold rare and historical materials that most students never explore. Some standout collections:

  • New Jersey Labor History Archives – Union documents, strike records, and oral histories from the 1930s–1980s.
  • African American Cultural Center – Manuscripts, photographs, and oral histories of NJ’s Black community.
  • Music Archives – Original sheet music from the 1800s, including works by early American composers.
  • University Archives – Historical yearbooks, student newspapers, and presidential records.

How to Access Special Collections:

  1. Request materials via MSU’s Special Collections Portal.
  2. Schedule a research appointment with a librarian specialist.
  3. Check digitized collections (some are available online!).

Common Mistake: Assuming Special Collections are only for history majors. Even business, STEM, and art students can find unique primary sources for their work.

Montclair State Libraries host free workshops on:
