Chapter 1: Getting Started – Your First Steps at Montclair State Libraries

1.1 How to Access the Libraries: Physical & Digital Options

Montclair State University Libraries offer multiple access points to ensure you can study, research, and borrow materials anytime, anywhere.

Physical Locations (On-Campus)

  • Library North (Main Campus) – The largest branch, housing general collections, special collections, and group study rooms.
  • Library South – Focuses on STEM, business, and interdisciplinary research, with specialized databases for engineering, computer science, and economics.
  • The Learning Commons (Library North) – A collaborative space with printing, scanning, and tech support for students.

Digital Access (Off-Campus & Remote)

  • Montclair State University Libraries Website (lib.montclair.edu) – Your one-stop portal for:
    • E-books & e-journals (accessible via ProQuest, JSTOR, and EBSCOhost).
    • Research databases (including Google Scholar, PubMed, and IEEE Xplore).
    • Interlibrary Loan (ILL) – Borrow books from other universities if Montclair doesn’t have them.
  • LibApps (Library Services Portal) – A mobile-friendly platform for:
    • Renewing loans
    • Checking due dates
    • Accessing your library account

Pro Tip: Bookmark the Montclair State Libraries FAQ for quick troubleshooting.

1.2 Your Library Card: How to Get It & What It Entitles You To

Every Montclair State student, faculty, and staff member automatically gets a library card upon enrollment. Here’s how to use it effectively:

What You Can Do with Your Library Card

✅ Borrow physical books (most loans are 30 days with renewals allowed). ✅ Check out laptops, tablets, and audio-visual equipment (e.g., cameras, microphones). ✅ Use 3D printers & scanners (with faculty/staff approval). ✅ Access restricted collections (e.g., rare books, archives, and special collections).

How to Renew or Return Items

  • Online: Use LibApps to renew loans before they’re due.
  • In-Person: Return books at any library desk or book drop.
  • Fines & Overdue Policies:
    • $0.25 per day for overdue books.
    • $5 replacement fee for lost/damaged items.

Real-World Example: A graduate student in the Education Department needed a rare 19th-century textbook for her thesis. Since Montclair’s Special Collections had a copy, she requested it via LibApps, and within 48 hours, it was ready for pickup. Without knowing about this service, she might have had to wait weeks for an interlibrary loan.

Chapter 2: Mastering Research at Montclair State Libraries

Research can be overwhelming, but Montclair State’s libraries provide tools and guides to streamline the process.

2.1 Finding Books & E-Books: The Right Way

Step 1: Use the Library Catalog (MontclairSearch)

  • Where to find it: https://lib.montclair.edu/find
  • How to search effectively:
    • Use keywords (e.g., "climate change policies" instead of "global warming").
    • Filter by format (books, e-books, articles).
    • Check "Available Online" for instant access.

Step 2: Explore E-Book Platforms

Montclair offers multiple e-book databases, including:

  • Ebrary (now ProQuest Ebook Central) – 200,000+ e-books on all subjects.
  • JSTOR – Academic journals & primary sources in humanities & social sciences.
  • EBSCOhost – Reference books & encyclopedias (e.g., CQ Researcher, Oxford Reference).

Actionable Tip: *If you can’t find a book in Montclair’s catalog, use WorldCat (worldcat.org) to check other university libraries and request it via Interlibrary Loan (ILL).*

2.3 Using Research Guides & Librarians for Help

Montclair’s librarians specialize in different subjects and create subject-specific research guides to help you:

Chapter 4: Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Even experienced students make avoidable mistakes when using academic libraries. Here’s how to stay ahead.

4.1 Mistake #1: Not Using Subject-Specific Databases

What’s Wrong: Many students only use Google Scholar, which misses Montclair’s premium databases (e.g., JSTOR for history, IEEE for engineering).

How to Fix It:

  • Check the research guide for your major.
  • Ask a librarian which databases are best for your topic.

4.2 Mistake #2: Ignoring Interlibrary Loan (ILL)

What’s Wrong: If a book isn’t at Montclair, some students give up instead of requesting it from another library.

How to Fix It:

  • Submit an ILL request via https://lib.montclair.edu/ill.
  • Turnaround time: 3-7 business days (faster than waiting for a local library).

4.3 Mistake #3: Not Taking Advantage of Librarian Consultations

What’s Wrong: Students don’t realize librarians can help with:

  • Narrowing down topics
  • Finding primary sources
  • Evaluating sources for credibility

How to Fix It:

  • Book a 30-minute consultation (https://lib.montclair.edu/ask).
  • Ask specific questions (e.g., "How do I find peer-reviewed articles on X?").

4.4 Mistake #4: Overlooking Digital Accessibility Tools

What’s Wrong: Some students struggle with e-books or PDFs due to reading disabilities or accessibility issues.

How to Fix It:

  • **Use Read&Write (text-to-speech tool) – https://www.texthelp.com.
  • Adjust font size & contrast in LibApps.
  • Request alternative formats (e.g., DAISY audiobooks).

4.5 Mistake #5: Not Renewing Loans in Time

What’s Wrong: Students forget to renew books, leading to late fees or lost access.

How to Fix It:

  • Set a reminder in LibApps for 10 days before due date.
  • Renew early if you need more time.
