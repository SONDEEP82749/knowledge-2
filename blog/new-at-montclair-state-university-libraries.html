**Narrow results by:** - **Material type** (Books, Articles, E-books, etc.) - **Publication date** (Limit to **last 5 years** for current research) - **Source type** (Peer-reviewed journals, Open Access, etc.) - **Subject keywords** (Helps find **relevant books** even if the title doesn’t match) **Example Search:** Instead of searching *"climate change"* (which returns **millions of results**), try: - **"climate change AND policy"** (for policy-focused articles) - **"climate change" filetype:pdf** (to find **PDFs** only) #### **Actionable Strategy #4: Save & Organize Your Searches** - **Create an account** in OneSearch to **save searches, bookmark articles, and set up alerts**. - **Use the "Cite" button** to **automatically generate citations** in **APA, MLA, or Chicago** format. **Common Mistake & Fix:** ❌ *Searching too broadly* (e.g., just typing *"machine learning"*) leads to **irrelevant results**. ✅ *Use **Boolean operators** (AND, OR, NOT) and **quotation marks** for exact phrases.* --- ### **2.2 Subject-Specific Databases: When OneSearch Isn’t Enough** While OneSearch is great for **general searches**, **specialized databases** provide **deeper, more curated content**. #### **Actionable Strategy #5: Know Which Database to Use for Your Field** | **Subject** | **Best Database** | **Why?** | |-------------------|---------------------------|----------| | **Business** | **Business Source Premier** | Full-text **peer-reviewed journals** on economics, marketing, etc. | | **STEM** | **ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore** | **Research papers, technical reports** | | **Humanities** | **JSTOR, Project MUSE** | **Academic journals** in literature, history, philosophy | | **Health Sciences** | **CINAHL, PubMed** | **Nursing, medicine, public health** studies | | **Education** | **ERIC (via EBSCO)** | **Research on teaching methods, curriculum** | **Real-World Example:** *A computer science major, James, needed **cutting-edge research** on **quantum computing**.
