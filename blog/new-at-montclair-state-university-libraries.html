Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Library Resources

While Montclair State University Libraries offer a wealth of resources and services, there are common mistakes to avoid when using these resources. These include:

  • Insufficient planning**: Failing to develop a research plan can lead to inefficient use of time and resources.
  • Inadequate citation management**: Neglecting to properly cite sources can result in academic integrity issues and decreased credibility.
  • Poor time management**: Failing to allocate sufficient time for research and study can lead to stress, decreased productivity, and lower academic achievement.
  • Ineffective database searching**: Using inadequate search terms or failing to utilize advanced database features can result in missed sources and decreased research quality.
  • Lack of feedback**: Failing to provide feedback on library services and resources can limit opportunities for growth and improvement.
