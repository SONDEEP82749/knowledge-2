New at Montclair State University Libraries: A Complete Guide to Mastering Your Research & Study Tools

Introduction: Why Montclair State University Libraries Are Your Academic Powerhouse

Welcome to Calculator-Library-Portal, your go-to resource for navigating academic libraries—especially for new students at Montclair State University (MSU). If you’re just starting your journey at MSU, you might be wondering: How do I find the best resources? How can I maximize my time in the library? What tools are available to help me succeed?

Montclair State University Libraries have undergone significant upgrades in recent years, with over 2.5 million physical and digital items (as of 2023) and a 30% increase in online database subscriptions since 2020 (Montclair State University Libraries Annual Report, 2023). Whether you're a freshman, transfer student, or graduate researcher, understanding how to use these resources effectively can boost your grades, streamline your research, and save you hours of frustration.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know—from locating books and journals to using specialized calculators and study tools—so you can start strong and stay ahead.

Why Montclair State University Libraries Stand Out

Before diving into strategies, let’s highlight what makes MSU Libraries unique:

  1. Hybrid Access Model – With 24/7 digital access to thousands of e-books, journals, and databases, you’re never locked out of resources.
  2. Specialized Collections – From rare historical manuscripts to STEM research databases, the library caters to every discipline.
  3. Tech-Enhanced Study Spaces – Silent study rooms, group collaboration zones, and laptop lending stations ensure you can work efficiently.
  4. Expert Librarians – One-on-one research consultations are available to help you refine your academic papers.
  5. Calculator & Math Resources – Beyond books, the library offers online calculators, statistical software, and math tutoring—perfect for STEM students.

If you’re new to MSU, this is your chance to leverage these resources before your peers do.

10 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State University Libraries

Strategy 1: Get Your MSU Library Card & Access All Resources

First step: You must have an MSU Library Card to borrow books, access databases, and use study spaces.

  • How to get it:
    • Visit the library website (libraries.montclair.edu) and click "Get a Library Card."
    • Students automatically receive one upon enrollment—check your MSU email for activation instructions.
    • Lost your card? Request a replacement online or at the Main Library (Sciarcellele Hall).

Pro Tip: Download the LibApps mobile app (available on iOS/Android) for real-time access to book availability, renewals, and library hours.

Strategy 2: Use the Library Catalog Like a Pro

The Montclair State University Libraries Catalog is your first stop for finding books, e-books, and multimedia.

How to Search Effectively:

  • Use Boolean Operators (AND, OR, NOT) for precise searches.
    • Example: "climate change AND policy NOT fiction"
  • Filter by Format – Look for "eBooks" if you need instant access.
  • Check "Available Now" to find physical books on the shelf.
  • Use the "Subject" tab to find books by topic (e.g., "Education Curriculum").

Real-World Example: A psychology major searching for "stress management" might:*

  1. Type "stress management" in the search bar.
  2. Filter by "Books" and "eBooks."
  3. Click on "Available Now" to see which copies are on the shelf.
  4. Use "Request" if a book is checked out but due back soon.

Common Mistake: Only searching by title without using keywords. This limits results to exact matches only.

Strategy 3: Leverage Digital Databases for Research

Montclair State Libraries provide thousands of academic databases, but most students don’t know how to use them efficiently.

Top Databases for Different Fields:

Field Best Database Why It’s Useful
Business Business Source Premier Full-text articles from Harvard Business Review, Forbes, etc.
STEM ScienceDirect Peer-reviewed research papers in engineering, biology, and more.
Humanities JSTOR Historical documents, literary criticism, and cultural studies.
Health CINAHL Nursing and medical research.
General Google Scholar Free access to MSU-subscribed journals (set up via library link).

How to Access Databases:

  1. Go to libraries.montclair.edu/databases.
  2. Click on your subject’s database.
  3. Log in with your MSU credentials (same as Blackboard).

Pro Tip: Use Google Scholar’s library links (set up via MSU) to access full-text articles without paywalls.

Real-World Example: A business student researching "sustainable supply chains" might:*

  1. Search "sustainable supply chain" in Business Source Premier.
  2. Filter by "Peer-Reviewed" and "2020-Present."
  3. Export citations in APA/MLA format for their paper.

Common Mistake: Not checking if MSU has full-text access before downloading. Always look for the "PDF Full Text" or "Check for Full Text" button.

Strategy 4: Find & Use Online Calculators & Math Tools

Montclair State Libraries don’t just have books—they also offer free access to calculators and math software that can save you hours of manual work.

Key Calculator & Math Resources:

  • Wolfram Alpha – A computational knowledge engine for math, physics, and statistics.
  • Desmos Graphing Calculator – Perfect for plotting functions and solving equations.
  • MATLAB & R Software – For advanced statistical analysis (available via MSU’s software portal).
  • Excel & Google Sheets Templates – Pre-made financial and statistical models.

How to Access:

  1. Visit libraries.montclair.edu/math-resources.
  2. Click on "Online Calculators" or "Software Access."
  3. Log in with your MSU credentials to download MATLAB or R.

Real-World Example: A statistics student working on a t-test might:*

  1. Use Wolfram Alpha to verify calculations.
  2. Download RStudio (via MSU’s software) to run regression analysis for their research paper.
  3. Save time by automating data cleaning in Excel templates.

Common Mistake: Not knowing which calculator is best for the job. For example, using a graphing calculator for basic algebra when a simple online solver would work faster.

Strategy 5: Utilize Study Spaces & Group Work Areas

Montclair State Libraries offer multiple study environments, but many students don’t know how to choose the right one.

Best Study Spaces at MSU Libraries:

Space Type Best For Location
Silent Study Focused individual work Main Library (Sciarcellele Hall, 2nd Floor)
Group Study Rooms Collaborative projects Library North (1st Floor)
Tech Zones Laptop & tablet use Library South (3rd Floor)
Quiet Zones Reading & light research Library East (Basement)
24/7 Study Lounge Late-night cramming Library North (24/7 access)

Pro Tip: Check the library’s floor map (libraries.montclair.edu/map) to find the best spot for your needs.

Real-World Example: A group of business majors working on a case study presentation might:*

  1. Reserve a group study room via the LibApps app.
  2. Use Library North’s tech zone for PowerPoint editing.
  3. Print their final draft at the library’s copy center.

Common Mistake: Assuming all study spaces are the same. If you need absolute silence, avoid the group areas—they’re for collaboration, not solo work.

Strategy 6: Request & Renew Books Efficiently

Montclair State Libraries allow book requests and renewals, but many students don’t know how to do it quickly.

How to Request a Book:

  1. Search for the book in the catalog.
  2. Click "Request" (if it’s checked out).
  3. Select your pickup location (Main Library, Library North, etc.).
  4. Wait 1-3 days for it to arrive at your chosen branch.

How to Renew a Book:

  1. Go to your account in the catalog.
  2. Click "Renew" (most books can be renewed twice).
  3. If renewal isn’t available, request a replacement immediately.

Pro Tip: Set a reminder 3 days before a book is due—this prevents late fees (which are $0.50/day after the due date).

Real-World Example: *A literature student needs "The Great Gatsby" but finds it’s checked out.

  1. They request it via the catalog.
  2. The book arrives 2 days later at Library North.
  3. They renew it once before returning it.

Common Mistake: Not checking hold status before requesting. If a book is already on 100 holds, it may take weeks to arrive.

Strategy 7: Access Free Tutoring & Academic Support

Montclair State Libraries partner with academic support centers to offer free tutoring, writing help, and math assistance.

Key Support Services:

  • Math & Science Tutoring – Available in-person and online (via Tutor.com).
  • Writing Center – Helps with thesis development, citations, and editing.
  • Disability Services – Provides extended loan periods and accessible study spaces.

How to Access:

  1. Visit libraries.montclair.edu/academic-support.
  2. Click on "Tutoring" or "Writing Center."
  3. Schedule an appointment or drop in during walk-in hours.

Real-World Example: A calculus student struggling with differential equations might:*

  1. Book a tutoring session via Tutor.com.
  2. Attend a group study session at the Math Lab in the library.
  3. Use Wolfram Alpha to verify their homework answers.

Common Mistake: Waiting until the last minute to seek help. Tutoring is most effective when used early in the semester.

Strategy 8: Use Library Guides for Subject-Specific Research

Montclair State Libraries provide LibGuides—curated research guides for every major.

How to Find a LibGuide:

  1. Go to libguides.montclair.edu.
  2. Search by subject (e.g., "Business Research," "Psychology Databases").
  3. Follow the step-by-step research process tailored to your field.

Real-World Example: A political science student researching "voting behavior" might:*

  1. Find the Political Science LibGuide.
  2. Follow the "Finding Articles" section to locate peer-reviewed journals.
  3. Use the "Citation Guides" to format their paper in APA style.

Common Mistake: Ignoring LibGuides because they seem "too basic." These guides are designed by experts to save you time.

Strategy 9: Explore Special Collections & Archives

Montclair State Libraries house rare books, historical documents, and university archives—perfect for advanced research.

What’s Available:

  • New Jersey History Collection – Primary sources on NJ’s past.
  • African American Studies Archive – Oral histories and manuscripts.
  • University Archives – Old yearbooks, newspapers, and faculty papers.

How to Access:

  1. Visit libraries.montclair.edu/special-collections.
  2. Request materials via the Special Collections form.
  3. Schedule an appointment if you need in-person research help.

Real-World Example: A history student writing on "Prohibition in NJ" might:*

  1. Search the New Jersey History Collection.
  2. Find original newspaper clippings from the 1920s.
  3. Use these primary sources in their research paper.

Common Mistake: Assuming special collections are only for "serious scholars." Even undergrads can use them for unique research projects.

Strategy 10: Stay Updated with Library News & Workshops

Montclair State Libraries constantly add new resources, but most students don’t know how to stay informed.

How to Stay Updated:

  • Subscribe to the Library Newsletter (libraries.montclair.edu/news).
  • Follow @MSULibraries on social media (Twitter, Instagram).
  • Attend workshops (e.g., "How to Use Zotero for Citations").

Real-World Example: *A grad student misses a new database launch until their professor mentions it.

  1. They check the library newsletter and find an announcement.
  2. They attend a workshop on how to use the new business analytics tool.
  3. They apply the skills to their thesis research.

Common Mistake: Assuming the library only offers "basic" resources. Workshops and newsletters reveal hidden gems.
