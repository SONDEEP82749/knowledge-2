Chapter 1: Getting Started with Montclair State University Libraries

1.1 Understanding the Library System: Branches & Services

Montclair State University Libraries consist of three main branches, each serving different academic needs:

  • The Library at the Center for Academic Success (CAS) – The largest branch, housing millions of books, journals, and digital resources, including specialized collections in business, education, and the humanities.
  • The Science & Engineering Library – A dedicated space for STEM students, featuring lab equipment, 3D printers, and specialized databases like ScienceDirect and IEEE Xplore.
  • The Music Library – A treasure trove for music majors, offering sheet music, audio recordings, and MIDI files, as well as recording studios for practice.

Pro Tip: Use the Library Website to explore branch locations, hours, and specialized collections before your first visit.

1.2 How to Access Your Library Account

Every Montclair State student receives a library card upon enrollment. Here’s how to set it up:

  1. Log in via the Montclair State Library Portal using your NetID and password.
  2. Borrow items (books, DVDs, etc.) by searching the Montclair State University Library Catalog.
  3. Renew loans online to avoid late fees—most items have a 28-day checkout period with automatic renewals if no one else requests them.

Real-World Example: A first-year business student, Jamie, struggled to find textbooks for her finance class. She logged into her library account, searched for "Principles of Finance" by Brealey, Myers, and Allen, and found a 2022 edition available for checkout—saving her $150 compared to buying a new copy.

Chapter 2: 10 Actionable Strategies to Master Montclair State Libraries

2.1 Strategy 1: Use the Library Catalog Like a Pro

The Montclair State University Library Catalog is your gateway to books, journals, and digital media. Here’s how to optimize it:

  • Use advanced search filters (e.g., "Peer-Reviewed Journals" or "Audiobooks").
  • Check the "Available Copies" tab to see which branch has the item.
  • Set up alerts for new arrivals in your field of study.

Common Mistake: Assuming all books are available at the main library. Many specialized titles are only at the Science & Engineering Library or Music Library.

2.2 Strategy 2: Leverage Digital Resources (E-Books & Databases)

Montclair State provides thousands of e-books and subscription-based databases, including:

  • JSTOR (for humanities & social sciences)
  • ProQuest (business, education, and health)
  • EBSCOhost (multidisciplinary research)

How to Access:

  1. Go to the Databases A-Z List.
  2. Select your subject (e.g., "Business" or "Psychology").
  3. Log in with your NetID to start searching.

Real-World Example: *A graduate student in Environmental Science needed a 2023 report on climate policy. Instead of paying for a PDF, she searched GreenFILE (via EBSCOhost) and found a free, full-text report from the UN Environment Programme.

2.3 Strategy 3: Schedule a Research Appointment with a Librarian

Montclair State librarians are experts in research methods and can help you: ✔ Refine your thesis topic ✔ Find credible sources (avoiding "Google Trap" results) ✔ Use citation tools like Zotero or EndNote

How to Book an Appointment:

  • Visit Library Research Help.
  • Select "Schedule a Research Consultation" and choose a librarian based on your major.

2.4 Strategy 4: Utilize Study Spaces & Group Work Areas

The libraries offer multiple study environments, each suited for different needs:

Study Space Best For Location
Silent Study Zones Focused individual work Main Library (2nd Floor)
Collaboration Tables Group projects Science & Engineering Library
Tech Lounge Laptop & device charging CAS Library (1st Floor)
Quiet Pods Private study sessions Music Library

Pro Tip: Reserve a study room in advance via the Library Room Booking System to secure a spot during peak hours.

2.5 Strategy 5: Borrow & Return Items Efficiently

  • Check out items at any branch (no need to return to the same one).
  • Use the "Hold" feature if a book is checked out—you’ll get an email when it’s available.
  • Return items via book drops (located outside each library) or self-checkout kiosks.

Common Mistake: Ignoring overdue notices. Late fees can add up—renew items early to avoid penalties.

2.6 Strategy 6: Access Interlibrary Loan (ILL) for Missing Resources

If Montclair doesn’t have a book or article you need, Interlibrary Loan (ILL) can get it for you in 3-5 business days.

How to Request:

  1. Search the catalog—if the item says "Not Available," click "Request via Interlibrary Loan."
  2. Fill out the form with your NetID and contact info.
  3. Wait for an email notification when it arrives (usually as a PDF download).

Real-World Example: *A history student needed a rare 19th-century document not held by Montclair. She requested it via ILL, and within 4 days, she received a scanned copy from a nearby university’s archives.

2.7 Strategy 7: Explore Special Collections & Archives

Montclair State’s Special Collections house unique historical materials, including:

  • Civil Rights Movement documents
  • Local New Jersey history archives
  • Rare books on literature & philosophy

How to Access:

  • Visit Special Collections.
  • Request appointments for restricted materials (some require librarian assistance).

2.8 Strategy 8: Use Library Technology & Printing Services

The libraries offer:

  • Scanners & copiers (free for students)
  • 3D printers (for engineering & design projects)
  • Audio recording studios (Music Library)

Printing Tips:

  • Print from any campus computer using your NetID.
  • Use the "Print Release" system to avoid wasting ink—release prints at a print station before printing.

2.9 Strategy 9: Attend Library Workshops & Events

Montclair State frequently hosts workshops on:

  • Citation management (APA, MLA, Chicago)
  • Data analysis with SPSS or R
  • Open Access publishing

How to Find Workshops:

  • Check the Library Events Calendar.
  • Sign up via Montclair State’s Canvas portal under "Library Workshops."

2.10 Strategy 10: Know Your Rights & Policies

  • Fair Use Policy: You can copy short excerpts for research (check Montclair’s Fair Use Guide).
  • Lost/Damaged Items: Replace or pay for repairs—$50+ fees apply for lost books.
  • Quiet Hours: 10 PM – 8 AM are silent in all branches.

Chapter 4: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About Montclair State Libraries

❓ Can I borrow books from other NJ universities?

✅ Yes! Montclair State participates in NJShare, allowing you to borrow from Rutgers, NJIT, and other NJ libraries with your Montclair ID.

❓ How do I find peer-reviewed articles?

✅ Use databases like:

  • JSTOR (humanities)
  • ScienceDirect (STEM)
  • PsycINFO (psychology) Filter results by "Peer-Reviewed" before downloading.

❓ What if I can’t find a book in the catalog?

✅ Try:

  1. Interlibrary Loan (ILL) for missing items.
  2. Google Scholar (then check if Montclair has access via Library Links).
  3. Ask a librarian—they may have access to hidden collections.

❓ Can I print from my laptop in the library?

✅ Yes! Use the "Print to Montclair" option in your browser settings. Then release prints at a print station using your NetID.

❓ How do I cite sources using APA/MLA?

✅ Use:

  • Zotero (free citation manager)
  • Purdue OWL’s APA Guide (link)
  • Librarian-led workshops (check the Library Events Calendar).
