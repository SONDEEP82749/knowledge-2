Actionable Strategies for Success

To make the most of the resources available at Montclair State University Libraries, consider the following strategies:

  • Develop a research plan: Before starting your research, define your topic, identify relevant sources, and create a timeline for completion. This will help you stay focused and ensure that you make the most of your time.
  • Utilize library databases: The libraries provide access to a wide range of databases, including academic journals, e-books, and online resources. Familiarize yourself with these databases to find relevant information for your research.
  • Seek research assistance: The libraries offer research assistance from experienced librarians who can help you navigate the resources available and provide guidance on your research topic.
  • Join a study group: Study groups can be an excellent way to collaborate with your peers, share knowledge, and stay motivated. The libraries provide study spaces that can be reserved for group study sessions.
  • Attend library workshops: The libraries offer various workshops and tutorials on topics such as research skills, citation management, and academic writing. Attend these workshops to enhance your skills and stay up-to-date with the latest research techniques.
  • Use library resources for cite management: The libraries provide access to citation management tools such as Zotero, EndNote, and Mendeley. These tools can help you organize your sources, format your citations, and create bibliographies.
  • Explore digital collections: The libraries have an extensive collection of digital resources, including e-books, online journals, and streaming media. Explore these collections to access a wide range of academic materials.
  • Participate in library events: The libraries host various events, including author readings, book discussions, and research seminars. Participate in these events to engage with the academic community and stay informed about the latest research trends.
  • Provide feedback: The libraries value feedback from students, faculty, and staff. Provide your feedback to help the libraries improve their services and resources.
  • Stay updated with library news: Follow the libraries' social media accounts and subscribe to their newsletters to stay informed about the latest developments, new resources, and upcoming events.
