New at Montclair State University Libraries: A Complete Guide to Maximizing Your Research & Study Experience

Introduction: Why Montclair State University Libraries Are Your Academic Powerhouse

Welcome to Montclair State University Libraries—a vibrant, resource-rich hub designed to support your academic journey from freshman year to graduation and beyond. With over 1.2 million physical and digital items (as of 2023) and partnerships with major databases like JSTOR, ProQuest, and EBSCOhost, the libraries offer unparalleled access to scholarly research, multimedia content, and specialized collections.

If you're new to Montclair State, you might be wondering:

  • How do I navigate the library’s vast resources?
  • What tools can help me ace my research papers?
  • How can I avoid common pitfalls that waste time and effort?

This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know—from library accounts and borrowing policies to advanced research strategies and hidden gems in the collections. Whether you're a first-year student, graduate researcher, or faculty member, this post will ensure you master the Montclair State University Libraries like a pro.

1. Setting Up Your Library Account: Your First Step to Success

Before diving into research, you need to activate your library account—your gateway to borrowing books, accessing databases, and requesting materials.

How to Create & Verify Your Library Account

  1. Visit the Montclair State University Libraries Website and click "Library Account" (usually found in the top-right corner).
  2. Log in using your Montclair State credentials (same as your university email).
  3. Complete any required verification (e.g., two-factor authentication if prompted).
  4. Check your account status—ensure there are no holds or fines blocking access.

⚠️ Common Mistake: Assuming your account is active just because you have a university email. Fix: Always log in manually to confirm your account is set up correctly.

What You Can Do with Your Library Account

  • Borrow books (physical and digital) with a 14-day loan period (extendable for faculty/staff).
  • Request interlibrary loans (ILL) for items not available at Montclair.
  • Access your checkouts, renewals, and fines online.
  • Set up alerts for new books in your field of study.

💡 Pro Tip: Enable email notifications for due dates to avoid late fees.

2. Exploring the Physical & Digital Collections: What’s Available?

Montclair State Libraries span three main locations:

  • Library North (main campus, largest collection)
  • Library South (specialized research & archives)
  • The Learning Commons (study spaces & tech support)

Physical Books & Materials

  • Over 1.2 million items, including:
    • Textbooks (check the Reserve Collection for course-specific materials).
    • Reference books (non-circulating but available for in-library use).
    • Special collections (rare books, historical documents, and local archives).
  • Loan periods:
    • Undergraduates: 14 days (renewable once).
    • Graduate/Staff: 28 days (renewable).
    • Faculty: 56 days (renewable).

Digital Resources (No Need to Visit the Library!)

Montclair’s online collections are 24/7 accessible from anywhere:

  • Databases: JSTOR, ScienceDirect, IEEE Xplore, and ProQuest Dissertations & Theses.
  • eBooks: Over 500,000+ via EBSCOhost, Gale, and ProQuest eBook Central.
  • Streaming Media: Kanopy, Alexander Street Press, and Films on Demand for documentaries and academic films.
  • Open Access Journals: Free scholarly articles via DOAJ (Directory of Open Access Journals).

🔍 Real-World Example: A political science student researching "Voting Behavior in New Jersey" can:

  1. Search JSTOR for peer-reviewed articles.
  2. Find eBooks on election psychology in ProQuest eBook Central.
  3. Access local election data from Montclair’s Government Documents Collection.

3. Mastering Library Search Tools: Beyond Google

Google is great, but library databases are optimized for academic research. Here’s how to search smarter:

Key Search Platforms & How to Use Them

Tool Best For Pro Tip
Montclair OneSearch Quick book & article searches Use filters (date, source type) to narrow results.
Library Catalog (MARC) Physical books & media Try subject searches (e.g., "Climate Change AND Policy").
JSTOR Humanities & social sciences Use advanced search for exact phrase matching.
ScienceDirect STEM & medical research Limit to "peer-reviewed" only.
Google Scholar Cross-disciplinary research Set Montclair as your library link in Scholar Settings.

8 Actionable Search Strategies

  1. Use Boolean Operators (AND, OR, NOT) to refine searches.

    • Bad: "Artificial intelligence"
    • Better: "Artificial intelligence" AND "healthcare" NOT "future trends"

  2. Leverage Subject Headings (found in catalog records) for more precise results.

  3. Check "Cited By" Links in databases (e.g., Google Scholar) to find related research.

  4. Save & Organize Searches using folder systems in JSTOR or Zotero (a free citation manager).

  5. Use "Get It @ Montclair" (a button in many databases) to instantly access full texts if available.

  6. Explore "Related Articles" in your database results for new research angles.

  7. Set Up RSS Feeds in JSTOR or Google Scholar Alerts to stay updated on new papers in your field.

  8. Ask a Librarian! The Research Help Desk (available via chat, email, or in-person) can save hours of frustration.

🚨 Common Mistake: Assuming "Google Scholar" is enough—it lacks full-text access to Montclair’s paid databases. Fix: Link Google Scholar to Montclair in Settings > Library Links.

4. Borrowing & Returning Materials: Policies & Etiquette

Loan Periods & Renewals

  • Books: 14 days (renewable once unless another user requests it).
  • Reserve Items: 2-hour or overnight loans (check course syllabus).
  • Faculty/Staff: Longer loan periods (up to 56 days).

📅 How to Renew:

  • Online: Via your Library Account.
  • In-Person: At the Circulation Desk.

Fines & Overdue Policies

  • Late Fees: $0.25/day per item (capped at $10).
  • Lost/Damaged Books: Replacement cost + processing fee.
  • Grace Period: Some items allow 1-day grace before fines apply.

💡 Pro Tip: Use textbook reserves to avoid buying expensive editions—many courses have free PDFs available.

Returning Books

  • Automatic Returns: If you’re near campus, some items auto-renew if not checked out by others.
  • Kiosks: Available for 24/7 returns (check for availability).
  • Lost & Found: Report missing items immediately to avoid fees.

6. Citation & Plagiarism: How to Avoid Academic Dishonesty

Plagiarism is not just copying—it includes:

  • Paraphrasing poorly (keeping the same structure).
  • Failing to cite indirect sources (e.g., quoting a source that quotes another).
  • Using AI tools without proper attribution.

How to Cite Correctly

Montclair supports multiple citation styles:

  • APA (Psychology, Education, Social Sciences)
  • MLA (Humanities, Literature)
  • Chicago (History, Business)
  • IEEE (Engineering, Computer Science)

📌 Best Tools for Citation:

Tool Best For How to Use
Zotero Free, open-source, great for large bibliographies Auto-import citations from databases.
RefWorks Cloud-based, integrates with Montclair Drag-and-drop PDFs for automatic formatting.
EasyBib Quick & simple Upload a Word doc for instant citations.

Common Citation Mistakes & Fixes

Mistake Why It’s Wrong How to Fix It
Missing page numbers Incomplete citation Always include page numbers (APA/MLA).
Incorrect DOI/URL formatting Broken links Use Zotero’s "Clean Up" feature.
Paraphrasing without rewording Still plagiarism Change sentence structure & vocabulary.
Using "et al." incorrectly Misleading Only use after 3+ authors (APA).

💡 Pro Tip: *Always double-check citations using Montclair’s Citation Guides or Purdue OWL (Online Writing Lab).*

8. Interlibrary Loan (ILL): When Montclair Doesn’t Have What You Need

If a book or article isn’t available at Montclair, Interlibrary Loan (ILL) can get it for you—for free.

How to Request an ILL

  1. Search Montclair OneSearch for the item.
  2. Click "Request via Interlibrary Loan" (if available).
  3. Fill out the form with your Montclair ID & contact info.
  4. Check your email for updates (usually 3-5 business days).

ILL Policies

  • Books: 3-4 weeks loan period (renewable if not requested by others).
  • Articles: PDF delivered within 24-48 hours.
  • No fines for lost ILL items (but report missing items ASAP).

🚨 Common Mistake: Assuming ILL is instant—some requests take weeks for rare items. Fix: Start early and follow up if delayed.
