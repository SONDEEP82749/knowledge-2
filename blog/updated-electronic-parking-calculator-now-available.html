Mistake 2: Overcomplicating the Pricing Structure

Problem: Too many discounts, tiers, and exceptions confuse users and reduce revenue rather than increasing it.

Solution:

  • Start with 2-3 simple pricing models (e.g., peak/off-peak, vehicle type).
  • Use the calculator’s simulation tool to test complexity before rolling out changes.
  • Example: A parking garage in Seattle introduced 12 different rate tiers and saw user confusion rise by 30%, leading to lower compliance.

Fix: Keep it simple—users respond best to clear, predictable pricing.

Mistake 3: Not Tracking & Analyzing Data

Problem: Many operators set rates once and forget, missing opportunities to adjust for seasonality, events, or market changes.
