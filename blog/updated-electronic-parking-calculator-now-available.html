Common Mistakes in Parking Management (And How to Avoid Them)

Even with the best tools, missteps can cost you money. Here are five frequent errors and how to prevent them:

Problem: Too many payment options (cash, cards, apps, RFID) confuse users. Solution: Simplify to 2–3 methods and track which works best.

Fix: ✔ Test different payment models in the calculator. ✔ Drop underperforming methods (e.g., if cash is only 10% of transactions, phase it out).

Fix: ✔ Include labor, utilities, and software costs in the calculator. ✔ Ensure your pricing covers costs + profit.
