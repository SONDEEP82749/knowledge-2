Problem: Many users assume flat-rate parking, but cities like London, Singapore, and NYC charge more during peak hours. Solution: Always check "peak vs. off-peak" settings and compare multiple times.

Mistake #2: Not Accounting for EV Charging

Problem: EV owners often forget to include charging costs, leading to underbudgeting. Solution: Use the EV charging estimator and compare home vs. public charging.

Mistake #3: Overlooking Discounts

Problem: Users miss AAA, corporate, or loyalty discounts, paying full price. Solution: Enter membership details and check for bulk discounts.

Mistake #8: Not Comparing Alternatives

Problem: Users stick to one parking option without checking cheaper alternatives. Solution: Explore "park-and-ride," shuttles, and public transit links.
