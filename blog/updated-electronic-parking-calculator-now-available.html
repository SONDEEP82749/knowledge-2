Common Mistakes That Inflate Parking Costs (And How to Avoid Them)

Even with the best calculator, human errors can still cost you money. Here are the biggest parking blunders and how to prevent them.

❌ Mistake 1: Ignoring Peak vs. Off-Peak Pricing

Why It’s Bad: Many drivers assume parking is the same all day, leading to unnecessary overpaying.

How to Fix It:

  • Always check the "Best Time to Park" feature before leaving.
  • Use a parking app (like ParkMobile) to see real-time rates.

❌ Mistake 2: Not Comparing Multiple Parking Options

Why It’s Bad: Sticking to the first garage you see often means paying more than necessary.

How to Fix It:

  • Run a side-by-side comparison in the Electronic Parking Calculator.
  • Look for "Parking Lots Nearby" on Google Maps for alternatives.

❌ Mistake 3: Forgetting to Account for EV Charging Fees

Why It’s Bad: EV owners only focus on parking costs, not charging, leading to hidden expenses.

How to Fix It:

  • Enable the "EV Mode" in the calculator.
  • Call ahead to ask about charging rates before committing.

❌ Mistake 4: Parking in High-Fine Zones Without Realizing

Why It’s Bad: Red zones, fire lanes, and no-parking signs can result in $100+ fines.

How to Fix It:

  • Use the calculator’s "Fine Risk Alert" feature.
  • Download local parking apps (e.g., ParkMobile) for real-time violations.

❌ Mistake 5: Not Using Parking Discounts or Subscriptions

Why It’s Bad: Many cities offer discounts for frequent parkers, but drivers don’t apply for them.

How to Fix It:

  • Check the calculator’s "Discounts" tab for local programs.

❌ Mistake 6: Overlooking Parking for Multi-Day Stays

Why It’s Bad: Daily parking rates add up—some hotels and garages offer weekly discounts.

How to Fix It:

  • Use the "Extended Stay" mode to compare daily vs. weekly rates.

❌ Mistake 7: Not Tracking Parking Receipts for Tax Deductions

Why It’s Bad: Business travelers miss out on tax write-offs for parking expenses.

How to Fix It:

  • Save all receipts in the calculator’s receipt tracker.

❌ Mistake 8: Assuming All Parking Garages Are Equal

Why It’s Bad: Some garages have hidden fees (e.g., tolls, valet, or "convenience charges").

How to Fix It:
