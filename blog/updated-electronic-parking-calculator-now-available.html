  2. Set tiered pricing (e.g., $2/hour during 7–9 AM, $1/hour from 10 AM–4 PM).
  3. Use AI recommendations to automatically adjust rates when occupancy exceeds 80%.

Common Mistakes When Using an Electronic Parking Calculator (And How to Fix Them)

Even with the best tool, mistakes can sabotage your success. Here’s how to avoid them:

Mistake 1: Ignoring User Feedback

Problem: If drivers hate the new system, they’ll avoid paying or find alternatives.

Solution: ✅ Conduct surveys (e.g., "How easy was your parking experience?"). ✅ Monitor app ratings and address complaints quickly. ✅ Offer a "help desk" for technical issues.

Mistake 2: Overcomplicating Pricing

Problem: If rates are too confusing, drivers won’t understand and may pay less.

Solution: ✅ Use clear pricing tiers (e.g., "Hourly: $2 | Half-hour: $1"). ✅ Display rates visibly (digital screens, app, and receipts). ✅ Offer a "simple pay" option (e.g., "Pay for 1 hour, extend later").

Mistake 3: Not Training Staff

Problem: If parking attendants or managers don’t know how to use the system, it creates chaos.

Solution: ✅ **Provide hands-on training for all staff. ✅ **Create a quick-reference guide for common issues. ✅ **Assign a tech support contact for emergencies.

Mistake 4: Failing to Monitor Revenue

Problem: If you don’t track earnings, you won’t know if the system is working.

Solution: ✅ **Set up automated reports (daily, weekly, monthly). ✅ Compare old vs. new revenue to measure success. ✅ Adjust pricing based on data
