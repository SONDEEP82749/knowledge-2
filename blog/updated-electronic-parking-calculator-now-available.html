Mistake: Failing to plan for electric vehicles leads to lost charging revenue and customer dissatisfaction.

Solution:

  • Dedicate 10–15% of spots to EV charging.
  • Use the calculator to model charging station ROI.
  • Example: A mall in Seattle added 20 charging ports and doubled its parking revenue by offering fast-charging discounts.

  • Adjust rates dynamically (e.g., higher during holidays, lower in winter).

4. Failing to Monitor and Maintain Parking Infrastructure

Mistake: Neglecting lot conditions leads to safety hazards and costly repairs.

Solution:

  • Use the calculator’s wear-and-tear analytics to schedule preventative maintenance.
  • Example: A parking deck in Chicago reduced repair costs by 50% by fixing cracks before they worsened.

5. Not Leveraging Data for Marketing

Mistake: Collecting data but not using it means missing upsell opportunities.

Solution:

  • Analyze customer behavior (e.g., who parks longest, who leaves early).
