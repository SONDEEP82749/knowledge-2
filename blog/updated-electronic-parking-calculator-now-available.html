Common Mistakes in Parking Management (And How to Avoid Them)

Even with the best tools, many businesses and cities make critical errors in parking management. Here’s how to avoid them:

Mistake 1: Ignoring Historical Data

Problem: Many operators don’t track past occupancy trends, leading to poor pricing and staffing decisions.

Solution:

  • Always input historical data into the calculator.
  • Compare "What-If" scenarios (e.g., "What if we lower rates by 20%?").

How to Fix It: ✅ Run monthly reports to spot patterns. ✅ Adjust pricing quarterly based on real data.

Mistake 2: Overcomplicating Pricing Structures

Problem: Some lots use too many pricing tiers, confusing drivers and reducing revenue.

Solution:

  • Start with 2-3 simple tiers (e.g., peak, mid, off-peak).
  • Use the calculator to test simplicity before adding complexity.

How to Fix It: ✅ A/B test pricing (e.g., flat rate vs. time-based). ✅ Keep it simple—drivers prefer clear, predictable pricing.
