Common Mistakes People Make (And How to Avoid Them)

Even with the best tools, missteps can lead to unexpected costs. Here are five major mistakes and how to prevent them.

Mistake 1: Ignoring Peak vs. Off-Peak Pricing

Why it’s bad: Many drivers assume parking costs are the same all day, leading to unnecessary expenses.

How to fix it:

  • Always check time-based pricing in the calculator.
  • If possible, arrive early or leave late to avoid peak rates.

Mistake 2: Not Factoring in EV Charging Costs

Why it’s bad: Some EV owners only calculate parking, forgetting charging adds $5–$20 per session.

How to fix it:

  • Use the EV-specific section of the calculator to compare home vs. public charging.
  • Look for discounted charging networks (e.g., Tesla, ChargePoint, Electrify America).

Mistake 3: Overlooking Hidden Fees

Why it’s bad: Some parking apps or lots charge extra for:

  • Reservations
  • Late cancellations
  • Transaction fees (3–5%)

How to fix it:

  • The calculator now flags hidden fees in its breakdown.
  • Always read the fine print before booking.

Mistake 4: Parking in Restricted Zones

Why it’s bad: No-parking signs, fire hydrants, and bus stops lead to $50–$200+ fines.

How to fix it:

  • The calculator maps restricted zones in your area.
  • Use Google Maps’ "Parking" layer to avoid risky spots.

Mistake 5: Not Comparing Multiple Options

Why it’s bad: Sticking to the first lot you see may cost 2–3x more than alternatives.

How to fix it:

  • The calculator compares 3–5 nearby lots at once.
  • Check parking apps (SpotHero, ParkMobile) for better deals.
