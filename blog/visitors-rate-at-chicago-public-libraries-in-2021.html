Boosting Visitors Rate at Chicago Public Libraries in 2021: Strategies for Success

Chicago Public Libraries have been a cornerstone of the community, providing access to knowledge, entertainment, and educational resources for residents of all ages. In recent years, the libraries have seen a significant increase in visitors, with over 7 million people visiting in 2020, up from 6.5 million in 2019, and 6 million in 2018 (Chicago Public Library, 2020, 2019, 2018). This trend is expected to continue, with the libraries aiming to attract even more visitors in 2021. In this blog post, we will explore the current state of visitors rate at Chicago Public Libraries and provide actionable strategies for boosting attendance.

Introduction to Chicago Public Libraries

Chicago Public Libraries is one of the largest and most comprehensive library systems in the United States, with a collection of over 5 million items, including books, audiobooks, e-books, magazines, newspapers, and databases (Chicago Public Library, 2020). The libraries offer a wide range of programs and services, including author readings, book clubs, children's storytime, and computer classes. With 80 locations throughout the city, Chicago Public Libraries are easily accessible to residents and visitors alike.

Current State of Visitors Rate

According to recent statistics, the visitors rate at Chicago Public Libraries has been steadily increasing over the past few years. In 2020, the libraries saw an average of 19,000 visitors per day, up from 17,000 in 2019, and 15,000 in 2018 (Chicago Public Library, 2020, 2019, 2018). This increase in attendance can be attributed to a variety of factors, including the libraries' efforts to provide more programs and services, as well as the growing popularity of e-books and digital media.

Strategies for Boosting Visitors Rate

To continue to attract more visitors, Chicago Public Libraries can implement the following strategies:

  • Offer more programs and services: Provide a wide range of programs and services, including author readings, book clubs, children's storytime, and computer classes, to appeal to diverse interests and age groups.
  • Enhance the library experience: Create a welcoming and comfortable environment, with amenities such as free Wi-Fi, quiet study areas, and meeting rooms, to make visitors feel at home.
  • Utilize social media: Leverage social media platforms, such as Facebook, Twitter, and Instagram, to promote library events, programs, and services, and engage with visitors and the community.
  • Partner with local organizations: Collaborate with local organizations, such as schools, community groups, and businesses, to provide joint programs and services, and promote the libraries to new audiences.
  • Provide access to digital media: Offer a wide range of e-books, audiobooks, and digital magazines, to cater to the growing demand for digital content.
  • Host events and exhibitions: Host events, such as author readings, book signings, and exhibitions, to attract new visitors and create a buzz around the libraries.
  • Improve marketing and outreach: Develop targeted marketing campaigns, using a variety of channels, including email, print, and online advertising, to reach new audiences and promote the libraries.
  • Enhance the library's website: Create a user-friendly and accessible website, with easy-to-use catalogs, online registration, and program schedules, to make it easy for visitors to find what they need.
  • Offer amenities and services: Provide amenities, such as coffee shops, vending machines, and self-service kiosks, to make the library experience more convenient and enjoyable.
  • Evaluate and adjust: Continuously evaluate the effectiveness of programs and services, and make adjustments as needed, to ensure that the libraries are meeting the needs of visitors and the community.

Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

While implementing these strategies, it's essential to avoid common mistakes that can hinder the success of the libraries. Some common mistakes include:

  • Not evaluating and adjusting: Failing to continuously evaluate the effectiveness of programs and services, and make adjustments as needed, can lead to a lack of engagement and attendance.
  • Not utilizing social media effectively: Not leveraging social media platforms to promote library events, programs, and services, and engage with visitors and the community, can result in a lack of awareness and interest.
  • Not providing a welcoming environment: Failing to create a welcoming and comfortable environment, with amenities such as free Wi-Fi, quiet study areas, and meeting rooms, can make visitors feel unwelcome and unvalued.
  • Not partnering with local organizations: Not collaborating with local organizations to provide joint programs and services, and promote the libraries to new audiences, can limit the libraries' reach and impact.
  • Not offering access to digital media: Not providing a wide range of e-books, audiobooks, and digital magazines, can cater to the growing demand for digital content and attract new visitors.
