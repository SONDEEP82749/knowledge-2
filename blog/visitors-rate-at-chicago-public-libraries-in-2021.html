Visitors Rate at Chicago Public Libraries in 2021: Understanding the Story

As of 2020, over 70% of Americans visited libraries at least once a year, making libraries an integral part of communities nationwide (Institute of Museum and Library Services, 2020). However, as of 2021, Chicago Public Libraries (CPL) reported a staggering 1.7 million visitors, marking a significant increase of 12% compared to the previous year (Chicago Public Library, 2022). In this blog post, we'll delve into the 2021 visitor rate at Chicago Public Libraries, exploring the underlying factors and actionable strategies behind this success.

An Overview of Chicago Public Libraries in 2021

With a collection of over 6 million items, 73 locations, and a vast network of resources, CPL is one of the largest public library systems in the United States. In 2021, the library witnessed a surge in visitors, driven by various initiatives and community programs. The data presented in this post highlights the trends and patterns observed in the 2021 visitor rate.

Recent Statistics: A Glimpse into the Past

  • 2021: 1.7 million visitors (a significant 12% increase)
