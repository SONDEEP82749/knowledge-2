8 Actionable Strategies to Maximize Efficiency with ASGA’s Calculators

1. Use the Signage Cost Estimator to Avoid Underbidding

Many signage businesses lose jobs because they lowball quotes to win contracts. ASGA’s Cost Estimator helps by:

  • Breaking down labor costs per hour (including setup, installation, and cleanup)
  • Factoring in material waste (critical for projects like vinyl or wood signage)
  • Including overhead (rent, utilities, software, and equipment depreciation)

Example: A local sign shop in Texas was losing 30% of jobs due to underpricing. After using ASGA’s estimator, they adjusted their quotes by 15-20%—now winning more contracts while maintaining healthy margins.

Pro Tip: Always add a 10% contingency buffer for unexpected costs (e.g., weather delays, material shortages).

  • Identify low-margin jobs (e.g., small vinyl decals) vs. high-margin projects (e.g., custom LED displays)
  • Adjust pricing dynamically based on material costs (e.g., aluminum vs. acrylic)

Common Mistake: Assuming all signage has the same profit potential. Fix: Use the analyzer to segment clients—e.g., corporate clients may justify higher premiums for custom designs.

Common Mistakes in Signage Cost Estimation (And How to Avoid Them)

Mistake #1: Ignoring Hidden Costs

What Goes Wrong: Many businesses only account for materials and labor, forgetting:

  • Permits (some cities charge $500-$2,000 for commercial signage)
  • Installation equipment rental (e.g., scaffolding, cranes)
  • Insurance (liability coverage for high-risk projects)

ASGA Fix: Use the Cost Estimator’s "Additional Fees" section to include all expenses.

Mistake #2: Overestimating DIY Savings

What Goes Wrong: Some signage businesses cut corners by doing installations themselves, leading to:

  • Damaged signs (e.g., improper mounting = voided warranties)
  • Safety hazards (OSHA fines for improper scaffolding)
  • Client dissatisfaction (poor installation = negative reviews)

ASGA Fix: The Profit Margin Analyzer helps you compare DIY vs. outsourcing costs—often, hiring a pro is cheaper in the long run.

Mistake #3: Not Factoring in Maintenance Costs

What Goes Wrong: Clients assume a sign is a one-time purchase, but:

  • LED signs need annual bulb replacements ($500-$2,000/year)
  • Vinyl banners degrade in sunlight (replacement every 2-3 years)
  • Digital displays require software updates ($300-$1,000/year)

ASGA Fix: The ROI Calculator includes maintenance projections to show true long-term costs.

Mistake #4: Underestimating Client Expectations

What Goes Wrong: Clients often expect custom designs without extra cost. This leads to:

  • Scope creep (e.g., "Can we add this logo for free?")
  • Unprofitable jobs (e.g., a $500 sign with $1,500 in custom work)

ASGA Fix: Use the Cost Estimator’s "Customization Add-Ons" to clearly label extra fees.

Example: A sign shop in Arizona lost $18,000/year due to unbudgeted custom requests. After implementing ASGA’s add-on pricing, they reduced scope creep by 60%.

Mistake #5: Not Tracking ROI After Installation

What Goes Wrong: Even if a client approves a sign, no follow-up means no proof of success. This hurts:

  • Repeat business (clients won’t invest again without data)
  • Referrals (word-of-mouth relies on tangible results)

ASGA Fix: The ROI Calculator includes post-installation tracking templates to measure:

  • Foot traffic increases (via Google Analytics)
  • Sales data (compare pre/post-sign periods)
  • Customer surveys (e.g., "Did you notice our new sign?")

Example: A restaurant chain used ASGA’s ROI tracker to show that their new awning sign increased takeout orders by 25%. They expanded the program to all locations.
