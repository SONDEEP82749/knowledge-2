What is the Spectral Index Library?

The Spectral Index Library in ArcGIS is a comprehensive collection of spectral indices that can be used to analyze and interpret remote sensing data. Spectral indices are mathematical formulas that combine different bands of satellite or aerial imagery to highlight specific features or characteristics of the environment, such as vegetation health, soil moisture, or water quality. The Spectral Index Library provides access to over 100 different spectral indices, each with its own unique application and use case.
