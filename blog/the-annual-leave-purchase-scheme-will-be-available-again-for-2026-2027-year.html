Introduction to the Annual Leave Purchase Scheme

The Annual Leave Purchase Scheme is a benefit offered by many employers, allowing employees to purchase additional leave days using their salary. This scheme is usually available for a specific period, and employees can choose to purchase a set number of days, depending on their employer's policy. The scheme is designed to provide employees with more flexibility and autonomy over their work-life balance, enabling them to take breaks, attend to personal matters, or simply enjoy some well-deserved time off.
