What Is the Annual Leave Purchase Scheme (ALPS)?

The Annual Leave Purchase Scheme is an employer-sponsored benefit that allows employees to purchase extra paid leave by deducting a portion of their salary. Unlike standard annual leave, which is typically 12.5 days per year (or 20 days for full-time workers in the UK), ALPS enables employees to extend their holiday allowance—often at a discounted rate—to accommodate personal, financial, or career-related needs.

Key Features of ALPS

  • Flexibility: Employees can buy days, weeks, or even months of leave in advance.
  • Tax Efficiency: Some schemes offer tax-free or tax-reduced purchases (depending on local labor laws).
  • No Forced Use: Unlike some paid-time-off (PTO) systems, ALPS days do not expire unless specified by the employer.
  • Employer Approval Required: Not all companies offer ALPS, so employees must check their HR policies.

Who Benefits Most from ALPS?

  • Freelancers & Gig Workers – Those with inconsistent income may use ALPS to secure guaranteed time off.
  • Seasonal Employees – Workers in retail, hospitality, or agriculture can bank extra leave during slow periods.
  • Parents & Caregivers – ALPS can help cover extended parental leave or medical absences.
  • High-Performance Employees – Those who want to take sabbaticals or career breaks without losing income.

How the Annual Leave Purchase Scheme Works in 2026/2027

Step 1: Check Eligibility & Employer Policy

Before purchasing leave, verify: ✅ Is your company offering ALPS for 2026/2027? ✅ What is the purchase rate? (e.g., 1 day = £X, or 1 week = £Y) ✅ Are there limits? (e.g., max 5 days per year) ✅ Is there a waiting period? (e.g., must work 6 months before eligibility)

Example: Company X allows employees to buy up to 3 extra days per year at £150 per day, with a minimum purchase of £450 (3 days).

Step 2: Understand the Cost Structure

Most ALPS schemes follow one of these models:

Purchase Model Description Example
Fixed Rate A set price per day (e.g., £100/day). Buying 5 days = £500 total.
Percentage-Based Costs X% of your daily salary. If daily pay = £120, and ALPS is 80% of salary, then 1 day = £96.
Tiered Pricing Discounts for bulk purchases (e.g., 10% off for 5+ days). 5 days = £475 (instead of £500).

Real-World Example: Yahoo Finance (2025) reported that TechCorp, a mid-sized IT firm, offers a hybrid model where employees can buy 1-3 days at £125/day or 4+ days at £110/day (10% discount).

Step 3: Calculate the True Cost vs. Value

Before committing, compare ALPS to other leave options:

  • Standard Annual Leave: Typically 20 days (UK) + bank holidays.
  • Sick Leave: Usually unpaid or partially paid.
  • Parental Leave: Statutory pay (£172.48/week, 2026/27) vs. ALPS flexibility.

Example Calculation:

  • Your daily salary: £150
  • ALPS rate: £120/day
  • Standard leave: 20 days = £3,000
  • ALPS purchase (5 days): £600
  • Net gain: 5 extra days for £600 vs. £3,000 for 20 days → Highly cost-effective!
