8 Actionable Strategies for Smarter Zoning and Planning

To get the most out of the Land Use Regulation Library, we've compiled the following actionable strategies for smarter zoning and planning:

  • Conduct a zoning audit: Use the library to review and analyze your city's zoning ordinances, identifying areas where updates or revisions may be necessary to promote more efficient land use.
  • Engage with the community: Utilize the library's resources to educate residents, businesses, and stakeholders about the importance of land use regulation and involve them in the planning process.
  • Implement form-based zoning: Consider adopting form-based zoning codes, which focus on the physical form and character of buildings and public spaces, rather than traditional use-based zoning.
  • Encourage mixed-use development: Use the library to research and develop policies that support mixed-use development, reducing the need for lengthy commutes and promoting more vibrant, walkable neighborhoods.
  • Promote affordable housing: Leverage the library's resources to identify and implement strategies for increasing the supply of affordable housing, such as inclusionary zoning and community land trusts.
  • Implement green infrastructure: Use the library to research and develop policies that incorporate green infrastructure, such as green roofs, rain gardens, and urban forestry initiatives, to mitigate the urban heat island effect and improve air quality.
  • Foster public-private partnerships: Utilize the library's resources to identify opportunities for collaboration between public and private sector entities, driving investment and innovation in urban development projects.
  • Monitor and evaluate progress: Regularly track and assess the effectiveness of your land use regulation strategies, using data and metrics to inform future decision-making.
  • Stay up-to-date with industry trends: Continuously update your knowledge and skills by attending conferences, workshops, and webinars, and participating in online forums and discussion groups.
  • Develop a comprehensive plan: Use the library's resources to create a comprehensive plan that integrates land use, transportation, and economic development strategies, ensuring a more cohesive and sustainable approach to urban planning.

Common Mistakes to Avoid in Land Use Regulation

While the Land Use Regulation Library offers a wealth of resources and opportunities for improvement, there are also common mistakes to avoid in land use regulation. These include:

  • Failing to engage with the community: Neglecting to involve residents, businesses, and stakeholders in the planning process can lead to mistrust, resistance, and ultimately, ineffective land use regulation.
  • Ignoring the needs of diverse populations: Failing to consider the unique needs and perspectives of diverse populations, such as low-income households, people of color, and individuals with disabilities, can result in zoning and planning decisions that exacerbate existing social and economic inequalities.
  • Not accounting for climate change: Land use regulation strategies that fail to address the impacts of climate change, such as sea-level rise, extreme weather events, and heat islands, can put communities at risk and undermine their long-term sustainability.
  • Over-relying on zoning as a tool for social policy: While zoning can be an effective tool for promoting social goals, such as affordable housing and community development, over-relying on zoning can lead to unintended consequences, such as displacement and gentrification.
