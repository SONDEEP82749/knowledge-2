Selecting the right supplier isn’t just about picking the most popular name—it’s about aligning with your practice’s needs, budget, and long-term goals. Here are 8 proven strategies to make the best choice:

🔍 Strategy 1: Define Your Practice’s Core Needs

Before comparing suppliers, audit your current workflows. Ask:

  • What are your biggest pain points? (e.g., lack of digital tools, high printing costs)
  • What therapeutic modalities do you specialize in? (CBT, DBT, trauma, etc.)
  • Do you serve a specific population? (teens, veterans, LGBTQ+, corporate clients)
  • What’s your budget? (Are you looking for bulk discounts or subscription models?)
