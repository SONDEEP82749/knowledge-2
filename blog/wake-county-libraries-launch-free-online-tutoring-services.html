Actionable Strategies for Using Online Tutoring Services

To get the most out of the Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, consider the following strategies:

  • Set clear goals: Before starting a tutoring session, define what you want to achieve and communicate your goals to the tutor.
  • Prepare in advance: Come prepared with specific questions, concepts, or assignments you need help with, and have all necessary materials ready.
  • Be active and engaged: Participate fully in the tutoring session, asking questions and seeking clarification when needed.
  • Follow up regularly: Schedule regular tutoring sessions to ensure consistent support and progress towards your goals.
  • Communicate with your tutor: Provide feedback to your tutor on what is working and what areas need improvement.
  • Use additional resources: Supplement your tutoring sessions with additional online resources, such as video tutorials, practice exercises, and study guides.
  • Stay organized: Keep track of your tutoring schedule, assignments, and progress to ensure you stay on top of your work.
  • Seek help when needed: Don't hesitate to ask for help when you need it – that's what the tutoring services are there for.
  • Review and reflect: Regularly review what you've learned and reflect on what's working and what areas need improvement.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Online Tutoring Services
