8 Actionable Strategies for Using Online Tutoring Services

To get the most out of the online tutoring services offered by Wake County libraries, students should follow these actionable strategies:

  • Set clear goals and objectives: Before starting an online tutoring session, students should define what they want to achieve and what they hope to learn. This will help them stay focused and motivated throughout the session.
  • Choose the right tutor: Students should select a tutor who is experienced in the subject they need help with and has a teaching style that suits their learning needs.
  • Prepare for the session: Students should review their notes and textbooks before the session, and come prepared with questions and topics they want to discuss.
  • Communicate effectively: Students should communicate clearly and effectively with their tutor, asking questions and seeking clarification when needed.
  • Practice active learning: Students should engage actively in the learning process, taking notes and participating in discussions.
  • Review and follow up: After the session, students should review what they learned and follow up with their tutor to ask additional questions or seek further clarification.
  • Use technology to your advantage: Students should take advantage of the technology used in online tutoring sessions, such as video conferencing and screen sharing, to enhance their learning experience.
  • Seek feedback: Students should ask their tutor for feedback on their progress and use it to improve their understanding of the subject.
  • Stay consistent: Students should schedule regular online tutoring sessions to stay on track and make consistent progress.
  • Take breaks: Students should take breaks during the session to avoid burnout and stay focused.
