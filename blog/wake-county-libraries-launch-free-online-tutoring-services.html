Once logged in, users can browse available tutors by subject, grade level, or language preference.

5. Technology Requirements

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Wake County’s Tutoring Services

While the program is highly effective, some users unintentionally sabotage their progress due to poor habits. Here are 5 mistakes to avoid:

1. Waiting Until the Last Minute

Mistake: Some students only seek help when they’re failing. Why It Fails:

  • Tutors can’t undo months of missed learning in a few sessions.
  • Test anxiety increases when cramming. Solution:
  • Start early—even one session per month makes a difference.

2. Not Being Specific with Questions

Mistake: Asking vague questions like "I don’t get this chapter." Why It Fails:

  • Tutors can’t guess what you’re struggling with.
  • Wastes time when the tutor has to ask follow-up questions. Solution:
  • Be precise: "Can you explain how to balance chemical equations?"

3. Skipping Preparation Before Sessions

Mistake: Showing up to a tutoring session without notes or questions. Why It Fails:

  • Tutors can’t diagnose problems if they don’t know what you’ve tried.
  • Less productive than if you had prepared. Solution:
  • Write down 2–3 key questions before each session.

4. Ignoring Supplemental Resources

Mistake: Only relying on the tutor without using extra materials. Why It Fails:

  • Tutors can’t replace textbooks or practice problems.
  • Learning plateaus if you don’t reinforce concepts. Solution:
  • Ask tutors for recommended books, videos, or practice tests.

5. Giving Up Too Soon

Mistake: Expecting instant results and quitting after 1–2 sessions. Why It Fails:

  • Mastery takes time—some concepts require multiple explanations.
  • Motivation drops if progress isn’t seen quickly. Solution:
  • Commit to at least 4–6 sessions before assessing results.
