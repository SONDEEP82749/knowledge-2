How Wake County’s Free Online Tutoring Works: A Step-by-Step Guide

Step 1: Accessing the Platform

To start, visit the Wake County Public Library’s Tutoring Portal (or search "Wake County Library Tutoring" in your browser). You’ll need:

  • A valid Wake County library card (or a free digital pass if you don’t have one).
  • A device with internet access (computer, tablet, or smartphone).
  • A quiet, distraction-free space for focused sessions.

Pro Tip: If you don’t have a library card, sign up online—it’s free and takes less than 5 minutes.

Step 2: Choosing Your Tutoring Method

Wake County offers three main ways to get help:

  1. Live Video Tutoring – Real-time sessions with a tutor via Zoom or Google Meet.
  2. 24/7 Chat Support – Instant messaging with tutors for quick questions.
  3. Phone Tutoring – Call-in sessions for those who prefer voice guidance.

Best for:

  • Live Video → Complex subjects (calculus, chemistry).
  • Chat → Quick homework help (algebra problems, grammar questions).
  • Phone → Last-minute test prep (SAT/ACT strategies).

Step 3: Scheduling or Dropping In

  • Scheduled Sessions: Book a tutor 1-7 days in advance (ideal for long-term help).
  • Drop-In Help: Available daily from 1 PM–9 PM (perfect for urgent questions).

Pro Tip: For math and science, schedule weekly recurring sessions to build consistency.

Step 4: Maximizing Your Session

During your tutoring time, engage actively: ✅ Prepare questions in advance (e.g., "Explain how to solve quadratic equations using the quadratic formula."). ✅ Use the whiteboard tool to work through problems together. ✅ Ask for alternative explanations if a concept isn’t clicking. ✅ Take notes—many tutors provide step-by-step summaries after sessions.

Common Mistake: Only asking for answers without understanding the process. Fix: Always ask, "How did you get that?" to reinforce learning.

8 Actionable Strategies to Get the Most Out of Wake County’s Tutoring

  • Use tutoring to clarify doubts that arise during group sessions.

Pro Tip: Wake County’s library branches often host virtual study group meetups—check their calendar of events.

3. Leverage the 24/7 Chat for Quick Doubts
