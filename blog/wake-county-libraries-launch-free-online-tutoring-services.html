Actionable Strategies for Students

To make the most out of the Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, students can follow these actionable strategies:

  • Set clear goals: Before starting an online tutoring session, students should set clear goals for what they want to achieve. This will help them stay focused and ensure that they make the most out of their time with the tutor.
  • Prepare in advance: Students should prepare in advance for their online tutoring sessions by reviewing their notes and textbooks, and making a list of questions they want to ask their tutor.
  • Be proactive: Students should be proactive during their online tutoring sessions, asking questions and seeking clarification on any concepts they do not understand.
  • Practice regularly: Students should practice regularly, using the skills and knowledge they have learned during their online tutoring sessions to complete their homework assignments and projects.
  • Seek feedback: Students should seek feedback from their tutors, asking for suggestions on how they can improve their performance and achieve their goals.
  • Stay organized: Students should stay organized, keeping track of their schedule and assignments, and making sure they have all the materials they need for their online tutoring sessions.
  • Communicate effectively: Students should communicate effectively with their tutors, clearly explaining their needs and concerns, and listening carefully to their tutor's feedback and guidance.
  • Take breaks: Students should take breaks, giving themselves time to rest and recharge, and avoiding burnout and fatigue.
  • Review and reflect: Students should review and reflect on their progress, identifying areas where they need improvement, and making adjustments to their strategy as needed.
  • Stay motivated: Students should stay motivated, reminding themselves of their goals and why they are working hard to achieve them.
