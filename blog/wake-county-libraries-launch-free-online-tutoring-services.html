How Wake County Libraries’ Free Online Tutoring Works: A Step-by-Step Breakdown

Before diving into strategies, it’s essential to understand how the program operates. Wake County Libraries’ tutoring services are seamlessly integrated with their digital platform, offering 24/7 access to expert tutors via live chat, video calls, and asynchronous messaging.

1. Accessing the Tutoring Portal

To begin, users must:

  • Visit the Wake County Libraries website (wakelibrary.org)
  • Log in to their library account (or create one if new)
  • Navigate to the "Learning & Tutoring" section under the "Digital Resources" tab

Once logged in, users will see three main tutoring options:

  • Brainfuse HelpNow – Live, on-demand tutoring for K-12 and college students
  • Tutor.com – Specialized help in subjects like SAT/ACT prep, resume writing, and career counseling
  • Homework Help (for younger students) – A simplified, interactive platform for elementary and middle schoolers

2. Choosing Your Subject & Tutor

The system allows users to:

  • Select their grade level or academic focus (e.g., algebra, biology, essay writing)
  • Choose a tutor based on availability (some sessions are first-come, first-served)
  • Schedule a session in advance (for test prep or long-term projects)

Pro Tip: For the best experience, book a session during off-peak hours (early mornings or late evenings) to avoid long wait times.

3. Connecting with a Tutor

Once connected, tutors provide:

  • Real-time guidance (via chat or video)
  • Step-by-step problem-solving (e.g., breaking down a calculus equation)
  • Study tips and resource recommendations (e.g., Khan Academy links for extra practice)

Key Feature: The platform includes a whiteboard tool, allowing tutors to visually explain concepts—just like a traditional study session.

4. Post-Session Support

After the tutoring session:

  • Recordings of sessions can be saved for review
  • Follow-up questions can be submitted via the portal
  • Progress tracking is available for long-term learners
