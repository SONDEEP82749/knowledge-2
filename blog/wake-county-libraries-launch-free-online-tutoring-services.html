8 Actionable Strategies for Maximizing Online Tutoring Services

To get the most out of the Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, students can follow these actionable strategies:

  • Set clear goals and objectives: Before starting an online tutoring session, students should identify what they want to achieve and communicate their goals to the tutor. This will help the tutor to tailor the session to meet the student's specific needs.
  • Prepare ahead of time: Students should review the material they will be covering during the session and come prepared with questions and topics they want to discuss.
  • Use the chat function effectively: The chat function can be a powerful tool for communicating with the tutor and asking questions. Students should use it to clarify any doubts or concerns they may have.
  • Practice active listening: Students should pay attention to what the tutor is saying and ask questions to clarify any concepts they don't understand.
  • Take notes and review them: Students should take notes during the session and review them afterwards to reinforce their learning.
  • Ask for feedback: Students should ask the tutor for feedback on their progress and use it to adjust their learning strategy.
  • Use additional resources: Students can use additional resources, such as online textbooks and educational websites, to supplement their learning.
  • Stay motivated and engaged: Students should stay motivated and engaged during the session by taking breaks and rewarding themselves for their progress.
  • Follow up with the tutor: Students should follow up with the tutor after the session to ask any additional questions or clarify any doubts they may have.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of the session: Students should evaluate the effectiveness of the session and provide feedback to the tutor to help them improve their services.
