Now that you know what’s available, here’s how to use these resources effectively to improve grades, test scores, and career prospects.

Strategy 1: Schedule Consistent Tutoring Sessions (Not Just Cramming)

Problem: Many students wait until the night before an exam to seek help, making it difficult for tutors to provide deep, meaningful assistance.

Solution: ✅ Block 30–60 minutes weekly for tutoring (e.g., every Tuesday and Thursday). ✅ Use Brainfuse’s "Schedule a Tutor" feature to book recurring sessions. ✅ Focus on weak areas first—don’t just ask for help on the day of a test.

Example: A Wake County high school senior, Jamie, struggled with chemistry but only sought help when her final exam was approaching. By the time she got a tutor, she was overwhelmed. Instead, if she had scheduled biweekly sessions to review concepts like stoichiometry and chemical reactions, she could have built confidence gradually.

Strategy 2: Use the Whiteboard & Tools for Visual Learning

Problem: Some students rely only on verbal explanations, missing out on visual and interactive learning.

Solution: ✅ Draw equations, graphs, or diagrams on the Tutor.com whiteboard to clarify concepts. ✅ Ask tutors to explain problems step-by-step while you follow along. ✅ Record sessions (if allowed) to review later.

Example: A 10th-grade student, Priya, was confused about linear equations. Instead of just listening to her tutor explain, she drew the slope-intercept form on the whiteboard, which helped her visualize the concept better.

Strategy 3: Leverage Brainfuse’s Skills Center for Self-Paced Learning

Problem: Not all students can attend live sessions due to scheduling conflicts or shyness.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Online Tutoring

While Wake County’s tutoring services are incredibly valuable, some users make costly mistakes that prevent them from getting the most benefit. Here’s what to avoid at all costs:

Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute

❌ Do: "I’ll ask for help when I’m stuck." ✅ Instead: Start early—tutors can prevent mistakes before they become big problems.

Example: A student who waits until the night before a test to ask for help may struggle with foundational concepts that tutors could have clarified weeks earlier.

Mistake 2: Being Too Passive in Sessions

❌ Do: "Just tell me the answer." ✅ Instead: Engage actively—ask why, not just what.

Example: Instead of saying, "What’s the answer to this problem?" ask, "How did you get to this step?" This deepens understanding.

Mistake 3: Ignoring Self-Paced Resources

❌ Do: "I only need live tutors." ✅ Instead: Combine live help with self-study (videos, quizzes, flashcards).

Example: Using Brainfuse’s Skills Center alongside live tutoring reinforces learning better than just one method.

Mistake 4: Not Setting Clear Goals

❌ Do: "I just want to pass." ✅ Instead: Define specific goals (e.g., "I want to improve my essay score by 10 points").

Example: A student who vaguely says, "Help me with math" will get less targeted help than someone who says, "I’m stuck on quadratic equations—can you explain the vertex form?"

Mistake 5: Not Following Up

❌ Do: "I’ll just ask once and forget about it." ✅ Instead: Review notes, practice, and revisit weak areas.

Example: A student who only asks for help once and never revisits the topic will forget what they learned.

Mistake 6: Overlooking Career & Soft Skills

❌ Do: "Tutoring is only for schoolwork." ✅ Instead: Use resources for career growth (resume help, interview prep, coding).

Example: An adult learner who only used tutoring for school missed out on LinkedIn Learning’s job search tools, which could have helped them land a better-paying job.
