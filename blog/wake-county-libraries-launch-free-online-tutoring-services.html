8 Actionable Strategies for Making the Most Out of Wake County Libraries' Online Tutoring Services

To get the most out of Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, students should consider the following strategies:

  • Set clear goals and objectives: Before each tutoring session, students should clearly define what they hope to achieve. This will help them stay focused and ensure that they're making the most out of their time with the tutor.
  • Prepare in advance: Students should review the material they'll be covering during the tutoring session and come prepared with questions and topics they'd like to discuss.
  • Be proactive and engaged: Students should be actively engaged during the tutoring session, asking questions and seeking clarification when needed.
  • Take detailed notes: Students should take detailed notes during the tutoring session, highlighting key concepts and ideas that they'd like to review later.
  • Practice consistently: Students should practice consistently, applying the concepts and skills they've learned during the tutoring session to real-world problems and assignments.
  • Seek feedback and guidance: Students should seek feedback and guidance from their tutors, using this feedback to adjust their approach and improve their understanding of the material.
  • Stay organized and manage time effectively: Students should stay organized and manage their time effectively, balancing their tutoring sessions with other academic and extracurricular responsibilities.
  • Review and reflect regularly: Students should review and reflect regularly, assessing their progress and identifying areas where they need additional support or review.
  • Communicate with tutors and librarians: Students should communicate effectively with their tutors and librarians, providing feedback and suggestions for how the online tutoring services can be improved.
