8 Actionable Strategies to Make the Most of Wake County’s Free Tutoring Services

1. Schedule Consistent Tutoring Sessions

Problem: Many students sign up but only use tutoring when they’re desperate—leading to last-minute cramming. Solution:

2. Prepare Questions Beforehand

Problem: Tutors can’t read minds—if you don’t know what you need help with, sessions become unproductive. Solution:

  • List 3–5 specific questions before logging in (e.g., "How do I solve quadratic equations?" or "Can you explain the difference between ‘affect’ and ‘effect’?").

3. Engage Actively During Sessions

Problem: Some students passively listen instead of participating, making tutoring feel like a one-sided lecture. Solution:
