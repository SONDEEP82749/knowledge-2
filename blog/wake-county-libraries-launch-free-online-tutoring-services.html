8 Actionable Strategies to Maximize Wake County’s Free Tutoring Services

1. Schedule Tutoring Sessions During Peak Productivity Hours

Not all students learn at the same time. Morning sessions (7–9 AM) are ideal for early risers, while evening sessions (5–9 PM) work better for night owls.

Pro Tip: Use Tutoring.com’s scheduling tool to book slots when you’re most alert.

2. Prepare a Clear List of Questions Before Each Session

Tutors can only help if you know exactly what you need. Before logging in:

  • Write down specific problems you’re struggling with.
  • Bring worksheets, textbook pages, or past assignments.
  • Note key concepts you need clarification on.

Example: ❌ "I don’t get math." ✅ "I’m stuck on solving quadratic equations using the quadratic formula—can you walk me through an example?"

3. Use the Whiteboard & File-Sharing Tools

Wake County’s tutoring platform includes interactive whiteboard tools where tutors can:

  • Draw graphs (for math/science)
  • Highlight key phrases (for writing assignments)
  • Share PDFs (for textbook problems)

How to Use It:

  • Upload a screenshot of your work before the session.
  • Ask the tutor to annotate directly on the whiteboard.

4. Record Sessions for Future Reference

If you miss something or need to review, most tutoring platforms allow recording. After the session:

  • Save the recording to your device.
  • Take notes on key takeaways.

Warning: Always ask permission before recording.

5. Leverage Test Prep Resources for Standardized Exams

Wake County offers specialized test prep tutors for:

  • SAT/ACT (Critical Reading, Math, Essay)
  • AP Exams (AP Calculus, AP Biology)
  • GRE/GMAT (for graduate school prep)

Strategy:

  • Practice past exams before tutoring.
  • Ask the tutor to focus on weak areas (e.g., time management, math formulas).

6. Join Study Groups via the Library’s Online Community

WCPL hosts virtual study groups where students can:

  • Collaborate on assignments
  • Discuss difficult topics
  • Get peer feedback

How to Join:

  • Check WCPL’s events calendar for live study sessions.
  • Use Discord or Zoom links provided in library announcements.

7. Use Brainscape for Flashcard-Based Learning

Brainscape, integrated into WCPL’s tutoring, offers spaced-repetition flashcards for:

  • Vocabulary (ESL, SAT words)
  • Math formulas (Algebra, Trigonometry)
  • History dates & events

Best Practice:

  • Create custom flashcards for your weak subjects.
  • Review them daily to reinforce memory.

8. Follow Up with Tutors for Long-Term Support

Some tutors offer follow-up sessions if you’re working on a long-term project (e.g., research paper, science fair experiment).

How to Request Follow-Up:

  • At the end of the session, say: "I’d love to revisit this topic next week—can we schedule another session?"

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute to Book a Tutor

Problem: Many students cram before exams and realize they need help too late.

Solution:

  • Book sessions at least 2 weeks in advance for test prep.
  • Use the "Waitlist" feature if no tutors are available.

Mistake 2: Not Using the Whiteboard or File Sharing

Problem: Some students only chat instead of using interactive tools.

Solution:

  • Always enable the whiteboard for math/science problems.
  • Upload documents before the session to save time.

Mistake 3: Expecting Tutors to Do All the Work

Problem: Students passively listen instead of engaging.

Solution:

  • Ask questions like: "Can you explain this step-by-step?" "What’s another way to solve this?"

Mistake 4: Ignoring Brainscape for Review

Problem: Some students only use tutoring but forget to reinforce learning.

Solution:

  • Spend 10–15 minutes daily on Brainscape flashcards.
  • Review past session recordings to fill knowledge gaps.

Mistake 5: Not Following Up with Tutors

Problem: Students book one session and never return.

Solution:

  • Schedule recurring sessions for long-term subjects (e.g., calculus, foreign languages).
  • Email tutors for additional resources (e.g., recommended books, YouTube channels).
