Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Even the best programs can fail if misused. Here’s what to avoid:

❌ Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute

Problem: Some students only seek help when exams are near, making it harder to grasp concepts. Solution:

  • Start early (e.g., use tutoring during the middle of the semester).
  • Break down large topics into smaller chunks.

❌ Mistake 2: Not Specifying What You Need Help With

Problem: Vague questions like "Help me with math" lead to wasted time. Solution:

  • Be precise (e.g., "I’m stuck on solving systems of equations—can you walk me through it?").
  • Bring specific problems to the session.

❌ Mistake 3: Skipping Preparation

Problem: Walking into a session without materials makes tutors guess what you need. Solution:

  • Prepare a list of questions before logging in.
  • Have your textbook or notes ready.

❌ Mistake 4: Ignoring Alternative Resources

Problem: Some students rely only on live tutoring and miss out on recorded lessons, chat help, or study groups. Solution:

  • Use all available tools (chat, forums, pre-recorded videos).
  • Combine methods (e.g., watch a video, then ask questions in a live session).

❌ Mistake 5: Not Following Up

Problem: After one session, students stop engaging, leaving gaps in learning. Solution:

  • Schedule regular sessions (e.g., every Tuesday and Thursday).
  • Review notes after each session to reinforce learning.
