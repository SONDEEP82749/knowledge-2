Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Free Online Tutoring

Mistake #1: Not Booking Sessions Early

Problem: Tutors are limited in number, and popular subjects (math, science) fill up fast. Solution:

  • Set calendar reminders to book sessions 2–3 days in advance.
  • Use the "Request a Tutor" feature if no slots are available.

Mistake #2: Being Passive in Sessions

Problem: Some students just watch while the tutor explains—without asking questions. Solution:

  • Take notes during sessions.
  • Ask for examples (e.g., "Can you show me another problem like this?").
  • Practice problems on your own before the next session.

Mistake #3: Ignoring the Writing Lab’s Limitations

Problem: The Brainfuse writing lab doesn’t check for plagiarism—only grammar and structure. Solution:

  • Use Turnitin or Grammarly for plagiarism checks.
  • Cite sources properly to avoid academic dishonesty.

Mistake #4: Not Using All Available Resources

Problem: Some students only use tutoring and miss out on free courses, e-books, and career tools. Solution:

  • Explore the library’s full digital catalog (OverDrive, LinkedIn Learning).
  • Attend virtual workshops (many are offered for free).

Mistake #5: Giving Up Too Soon

Problem: Progress takes time—some students quit after one bad session. Solution:

  • Commit to at least 4–6 sessions before assessing results.
  • Adjust strategies if a tutor isn’t a good fit (try a different platform).
