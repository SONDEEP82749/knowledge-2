How Wake County’s Free Online Tutoring Services Work: A Step-by-Step Breakdown

Wake County Libraries’ new virtual tutoring hub integrates multiple platforms to ensure 24/7 access to expert guidance. Here’s how it functions:

1. Platform Integration: Brainfuse, Tutoring.com, and Khan Academy

WCPL has partnered with three major tutoring platforms to provide specialized support:

  • Brainfuse – Offers live homework help, skill-building exercises, and writing labs for K-12 students.
  • Tutoring.com – Provides one-on-one tutoring sessions with certified educators in subjects like STEM, humanities, and test prep.
  • Khan Academy – Supports self-paced learning with video lessons, practice problems, and progress tracking.

2. Accessibility: How to Get Started

Students and parents can access these services without a library card (though one is recommended for full benefits). Here’s how:

  • Website Access: Visit Wake County Libraries’ Tutoring Page and log in via your Wake County Public Library account (or create one for free).
  • Mobile App: Download the WCPL app (available on iOS and Android) for on-the-go tutoring.
  • Phone Support: Call 919-856-4700 for assistance in setting up an account.

3. Who Can Use These Services?

The program is open to everyone, including:

  • K-12 students (public, private, and homeschooled)
  • College students (for research and writing assistance)
  • Adult learners (GED prep, career training, and language learning)
  • Parents and educators (for lesson planning and curriculum support)

4. Key Features of the Tutoring Services

Feature Description
Live Tutoring Sessions Real-time 1:1 or group tutoring with certified teachers.
SkillSurfer (Brainfuse) AI-powered practice exercises to reinforce learning.
Writing Assistance Expert feedback on essays, resumes, and research papers.
Test Prep Resources SAT, ACT, AP, and college entrance exam practice.
Language Learning ESL (English as a Second Language) support and conversation practice.
Career Help Resume reviews, interview prep, and job search strategies.

5. Cost: 100% Free for Wake County Residents

Unlike traditional tutoring services (which can cost $20–$50 per hour), Wake County’s program is completely free, funded by taxpayer dollars and library grants.

8 Actionable Strategies to Maximize Wake County’s Free Online Tutoring Services

Now that you know how the services work, let’s dive into how to use them effectively to boost academic performance.

Strategy 1: Schedule Tutoring Sessions During Peak Productivity Hours

Not all students learn at the same time. Research from the University of Illinois (2023) shows that morning sessions (8–10 AM) and late afternoon (3–5 PM) yield the highest retention rates.

How to apply this:

  • If your child is a morning person, book a 9 AM session for math.
  • If they’re a night owl, try a 7 PM slot for English.
  • Use the Tutoring.com scheduler to reserve preferred times.

Strategy 2: Use Brainfuse’s SkillSurfer for Self-Paced Learning

Brainfuse’s SkillSurfer is an AI-driven practice tool that adapts to a student’s learning level. Unlike passive video lessons, it actively engages learners with adaptive quizzes.

How to use it:

  1. Log in to Brainfuse via WCPL’s website.
  2. Select your grade level and subject.
  3. Complete short, timed exercises to reinforce concepts.
  4. Review mistakes with instant explanations.

Example: A 7th grader struggling with algebra fractions can take a 10-minute SkillSurfer quiz, then receive step-by-step corrections from a tutor.

Strategy 3: Leverage Khan Academy for Foundational Skills

While Tutoring.com is best for real-time help, Khan Academy excels at building foundational knowledge.

How to integrate both:

  • Use Khan Academy to master basic concepts (e.g., multiplication, grammar rules).
  • Then, book a Tutoring.com session to apply those skills in complex problems.

Real-world example: A high school senior preparing for the SAT can:

  1. Watch Khan Academy’s SAT Math videos on exponents.
  2. Practice 10 problems in SkillSurfer.
  3. Join a Tutoring.com session to debug mistakes in a timed practice test.

Strategy 4: Request Writing Lab Assistance for Essays & Research Papers

The Brainfuse Writing Lab is a hidden gem for students who struggle with structure, grammar, or citations.

How to use it:

  1. Upload a draft of your essay (or a single paragraph).
  2. A tutor will review it within 24 hours with detailed feedback.
  3. Revise based on suggestions and resubmit if needed.

Example: A college freshman writing a history research paper can:

  • Get feedback on thesis clarity.
  • Learn how to cite sources properly (APA/MLA).
  • Avoid plagiarism with tutor guidance.

Strategy 5: Use Tutoring for Test Prep (SAT, ACT, AP Exams)

Many students pay hundreds for test prep courses, but Wake County’s tutors can simulate exam conditions for free.

How to prepare:

  • Week 1: Take a full-length practice test (Khan Academy or official SAT/ACT materials).
  • Week 2: Book a Tutoring.com session to review mistakes.
  • Week 3: Focus on weak areas (e.g., algebra, reading comprehension).

Success story: A Wake County high school junior raised her SAT score from 1100 to 1350 in three months by:

  • Using Khan Academy’s SAT prep.
  • Getting personalized feedback from a tutor on math word problems.
  • Practicing timed essay writing in the Writing Lab.

Strategy 6: Parents & Educators: Use Tutoring for Lesson Planning

Teachers and parents can use tutors as a second opinion for lesson plans.

How to apply this:

  • If a student is struggling with a concept, book a group tutoring session (up to 4 students).
  • Use SkillSurfer data to identify class-wide weaknesses.
  • Adjust homework assignments based on tutor recommendations.

Example: A 4th-grade teacher notices half the class is confused about fractions. She:

  1. Books a group tutoring session with a math specialist.
  2. Gets strategies to simplify explanations.
  3. Assigns SkillSurfer exercises as homework.

Strategy 7: Adult Learners: Get Career & GED Help

Wake County’s tutoring isn’t just for students—adults can also benefit from:

  • GED prep (math, science, social studies).
  • Resume & cover letter reviews.
  • Job interview coaching.

How to access these services:

  1. Log in to Tutoring.com and select "Adult Learning Center."
  2. Choose your goal (e.g., "Prepare for GED Math").
  3. Schedule a 1:1 session with a career counselor.

Real-world example: A Wake County resident retraining for a nursing career used:

  • Brainfuse’s science tutors to review anatomy.
  • Writing Lab help to craft a strong nursing school application.
  • Interview prep from a career tutor.

Strategy 8: Track Progress with Analytics & Reports

Both Brainfuse and Tutoring.com provide detailed progress reports that parents and students can use to stay motivated.

How to use analytics:

  • Brainfuse: Check "My Progress" to see improvement in weak areas.
  • Tutoring.com: Review "Session Notes" from tutors for personalized feedback.
  • Khan Academy: Track "Mastery Levels" in different subjects.

Example: A middle schooler consistently scores below 70% on algebra quizzes. After three tutoring sessions, their SkillSurfer score jumps to 85%, and the tutor notes:

"Great improvement in solving linear equations! Keep practicing word problems."

Common Mistakes When Using Wake County’s Free Tutoring Services (And How to Avoid Them)

While these services are incredibly valuable, some users don’t maximize their benefits due to preventable mistakes. Here’s what to avoid:

Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute for Help

Problem: Many students only seek tutoring when they’re failing, making it harder to catch up.

Solution:

  • Use tutoring for prevention, not just last-minute fixes.
  • Example: If your child is struggling with fractions in 5th grade, get help now—don’t wait until algebra in 8th grade.

Mistake 2: Not Using All Available Resources

Problem: Some users only use one platform (e.g., Tutoring.com) and miss out on Brainfuse or Khan Academy.

Solution:

  • Combine all three for best results:
    • Khan Academy = Foundational learning.
    • Brainfuse = Practice & Writing Help.
    • Tutoring.com = Real-time expert guidance.

Mistake 3: Skipping the SkillSurfer Practice

Problem: Students book tutoring sessions but don’t do the practice exercises between sessions.

Solution:

  • Do at least 10–15 minutes of SkillSurfer daily to reinforce learning.
  • Example: If you have a math tutoring session on Wednesday, do SkillSurfer problems on Monday & Tuesday.

Mistake 4: Not Setting Clear Goals with Tutors

Problem: Some students go to tutoring without a plan, leading to wasted time.

Solution:

  • Before each session, define 1–2 specific goals:
    • "I need help with solving quadratic equations."
    • "I want to improve my essay structure."
  • Example: Instead of saying, "Help me with math," say:

    "I’m stuck on factoring polynomials—can we practice 3 problems together?"
