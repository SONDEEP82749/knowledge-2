To maximize the benefits of the free online tutoring services, students and parents can employ the following strategies:

### 1. Set Clear Goals and Objectives
Establishing clear goals and objectives is crucial for effective learning. Students should identify areas where they require additional support and create a plan to achieve their academic targets. By doing so, they can focus their efforts, prioritize their learning, and make the most of the online tutoring services.

### 2. Develop a Routine and Stick to It
Consistency is key when it comes to learning. Students should develop a routine that includes regular online tutoring sessions, practice exercises, and review of material. By sticking to a schedule, students can build momentum, reinforce their understanding of concepts, and make steady progress.

### 3. Engage Actively with Tutors
The online tutoring platform is designed to facilitate interactive learning. Students should engage actively with their tutors, asking questions, seeking clarification, and participating in discussions. By doing so, they can clarify doubts, gain a deeper understanding of concepts, and develop critical thinking skills.

### 4. Utilize Additional Resources
The Wake County libraries' online tutoring services are complemented by a range of additional resources, including educational videos, interactive simulations, and practice exercises. Students should take advantage of these resources to supplement their learning, explore topics in greater depth, and reinforce their understanding of concepts.

### 5. Monitor Progress and Adjust
Regular progress monitoring is essential for effective learning. Students should track their progress, identify areas of strength and weakness, and adjust their learning strategy accordingly. By doing so, they can refine their approach, address knowledge gaps, and optimize their use of the online tutoring services.
