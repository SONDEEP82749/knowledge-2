Actionable Strategies for Making the Most of Online Tutoring Services

To get the most out of Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, students should follow these actionable strategies:

  • Set clear goals and objectives: Before starting an online tutoring session, students should clearly define what they hope to achieve. This could be improving their understanding of a particular concept, completing a difficult assignment, or preparing for a test or exam.
  • Prepare ahead of time: Students should come prepared to their online tutoring sessions with all necessary materials, including textbooks, notes, and assignments. This will help to ensure that they make the most of their time with their tutor.
  • Ask questions and seek feedback: Online tutoring sessions are a great opportunity for students to ask questions and seek feedback from their tutors. Students should not be afraid to ask for help when they need it, and should be open to constructive criticism and feedback.
  • Practice active listening and engagement: Students should actively engage with their tutors during online tutoring sessions, asking questions and seeking clarification when needed. This will help to ensure that they fully understand the material and are able to apply it in practice.
  • Use technology to your advantage: Wake County libraries' online tutoring platform offers a range of features and tools, including video conferencing, screen sharing, and virtual whiteboards. Students should take advantage of these features to enhance their learning experience and make the most of their time with their tutor.
  • Follow up with additional practice: After an online tutoring session, students should follow up with additional practice and review to reinforce their understanding of the material. This could include completing homework assignments, reading chapters in a textbook, or working on a project.
  • Communicate with your tutor: Students should maintain open and regular communication with their tutor, providing feedback and updates on their progress. This will help to ensure that their tutor is aware of their needs and can provide targeted support and guidance.
  • Stay organized and manage your time effectively: Students should stay organized and manage their time effectively, prioritizing their assignments and tasks and making sure to leave enough time for online tutoring sessions and additional practice.
  • Seek help when you need it: Finally, students should not be afraid to seek help when they need it. Online tutoring services are available to provide support and guidance, and students should take advantage of these resources to achieve their academic goals.
