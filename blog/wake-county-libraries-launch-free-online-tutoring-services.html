How Wake County’s Free Online Tutoring Services Work

Wake County Public Library’s Tutoring@YourLibrary program offers real-time, one-on-one academic assistance via Tutoring.com, a trusted online tutoring platform. Here’s a breakdown of how it functions:

1. Eligibility & Access

  • Open to all Wake County residents (including students, parents, and educators).
  • No library card required—access is available through the WCPL website or via the Tutoring.com portal.
  • Subjects covered: Math, science, English, history, foreign languages, and test prep (SAT, ACT, AP exams).

2. Scheduling & Availability

  • 24/7 access to on-demand tutoring.
  • Live sessions available Monday–Friday, 10 AM–10 PM and Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM.
  • No appointment needed—students can connect instantly via chat, video, or voice call.

3. Who Are the Tutors?

  • Certified educators with expertise in K-12 and college-level subjects.
  • Background-checked professionals ensuring a safe learning environment.
  • Multilingual support for non-English speakers.

4. Additional Features

  • Homework help with step-by-step explanations.
  • Writing workshops for essays, research papers, and college applications.
  • College readiness resources, including essay reviews and test prep strategies.

Why This Matters Unlike traditional tutoring services that can be expensive or limited in availability, Wake County’s program provides free, high-quality support—making education more equitable for all students.

8 Actionable Strategies to Maximize Wake County’s Free Tutoring Services

1. Schedule Sessions During Peak Hours for Faster Response

Tutors are most available between 3 PM–7 PM on weekdays, when students are typically struggling with after-school assignments. Avoid rush hours (right after school or before exams) unless you’re prepared for longer wait times.

Pro Tip: Use the Tutoring.com app to check tutor availability in real time.

2. Prepare Questions Beforehand for Efficient Sessions

Tutors can only help so much in a single session. Come prepared with:

  • Specific problems (e.g., "Help me solve this quadratic equation").
  • Key concepts you’re struggling with (e.g., "I don’t understand photosynthesis").
  • Past assignments (if allowed) to get targeted feedback.

Example: Instead of: "I’m bad at math." Try: "Can you walk me through solving systems of equations using substitution?"

3. Use the "Homework Help" Feature for Step-by-Step Guidance

The platform offers interactive problem-solving tools where tutors can:

  • Draw graphs for math problems.
  • Highlight key parts of a text for English assignments.
  • Explain scientific concepts with visual aids.

Best For: Students who learn better with visual explanations rather than just verbal instructions.

4. Leverage Writing Workshops for College Applications

The Writing Center is a hidden gem for students applying to college. Tutors can:

  • Review personal statements for clarity and impact.
  • Help structure essays with strong thesis statements.
  • Provide feedback on grammar and style.

Real-World Example: A high school senior in Raleigh used the Writing Center to refine her common app essay, which helped her secure a full-tuition scholarship at a local university.

5. Take Advantage of Test Prep Resources

Whether preparing for the SAT, ACT, or AP exams, Wake County’s tutors offer:

  • Practice question breakdowns.
  • Time-management strategies.
  • Common mistake analysis (e.g., "Why did you lose points on this math section?").

Pro Tip: Use the test prep section to identify weak areas before full-length practice tests.

6. Encourage Group Study Sessions (If Available)

While most sessions are one-on-one, some tutors may allow small group discussions (e.g., for group projects). Ask politely if the tutor can facilitate a collaborative session with peers.

7. Follow Up with Tutors for Long-Term Learning

Some students benefit from multiple sessions on the same topic. Request a "follow-up" tutor if you’re still struggling after one session.

Example Script: "I worked on this algebra problem with you last week, but I’m still confused about the steps. Would you be available for a quick refresher?"

8. Combine Tutoring with Library Resources

Wake County libraries offer free access to:

  • Khan Academy (for math and science).
  • Mango Languages (for foreign language practice).
  • Gale Courses (for career and college prep).

Strategy: Use tutoring for real-time help and library resources for self-paced learning.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

1. Waiting Until the Last Minute for Help

Mistake: Some students only seek tutoring days before an exam, making it hard for tutors to cover everything.

Solution: Start early—even one session per week can prevent last-minute panic.

2. Not Using the Platform’s Full Features

Mistake: Many students only chat with tutors instead of using whiteboard tools, file uploads, or recorded lessons.

Solution: Explore all features—some tutors can record sessions for later review.

3. Being Passive During Sessions

Mistake: Some students just listen without asking questions or engaging.

Solution: Take notes, ask clarifying questions, and practice problems during the session.

4. Ignoring Follow-Up Resources

Mistake: After a tutoring session, some students don’t revisit the material on their own.

Solution: Use library databases (like Khan Academy) to practice what you learned.

5. Not Leveraging Multilingual Support

Mistake: Non-English speakers may avoid tutoring due to language barriers.

Solution: Wake County offers bilingual tutors—request a Spanish, Mandarin, or other language tutor if needed.

FAQ Section: Everything You Need to Know About Wake County’s Tutoring Services

1. Do I need a Wake County library card to use the tutoring services?

✅ No, you do not. The service is open to all Wake County residents, regardless of library card status. Access is provided through the WCPL website or Tutoring.com.

2. What subjects and grade levels are covered?

✅ The program supports:

  • K-12 subjects (math, science, English, history, foreign languages).
  • College prep (SAT, ACT, AP exams).
  • Adult learners (GED, career skills).

3. Can I use the tutoring service for college-level courses?

✅ Yes! Wake County’s tutors assist with:

  • Undergraduate-level math, science, and humanities.
  • Business and computer science basics.
  • Writing and research skills for college papers.

4. How do I schedule a tutoring session if I’m not available during peak hours?

✅ While live tutoring is best during peak hours, you can:

  • Use the "Homework Help" feature for asynchronous support.
  • Record sessions for later review.
  • Ask tutors to email explanations of concepts.

5. Is the tutoring service confidential?

✅ Yes. All sessions are private and secure, with no records kept of personal information beyond what’s necessary for the session.
