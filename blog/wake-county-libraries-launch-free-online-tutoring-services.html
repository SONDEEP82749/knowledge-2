Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Mistake 1: Showing Up Without Preparation

Problem: Tutors can’t magically fix gaps if you don’t engage with materials beforehand. Solution:

  • Complete 1–2 self-paced modules before each session.
  • Bring specific questions (not just "Help me with homework").

Mistake 2: Over-Reliance on Tutors for Homework

Problem: Tutors are guides, not doers—students must do the work themselves. Solution:

  • Use tutors for explanations, then practice independently.
  • Ask for feedback on your work, not just answers.

Mistake 3: Ignoring Self-Paced Resources

Problem: Many students skip the free quizzes and videos, missing out on reinforcement. Solution:

  • Spend 10–15 minutes daily on self-paced content.
  • Take progress quizzes to track improvement.

Mistake 4: Not Following Up After Sessions

Problem: Knowledge fades if not reinforced. Solution:

  • Summarize key points in a notebook.
  • Apply concepts in homework within 24 hours.

Mistake 5: Missing Deadlines for Scheduling

Problem: Popular tutors book up fast—last-minute requests often get denied. Solution:

  • Schedule sessions 1–2 weeks in advance.
  • Set calendar reminders for appointments.
