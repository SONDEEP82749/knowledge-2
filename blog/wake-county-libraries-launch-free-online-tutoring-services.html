How Wake County’s Free Online Tutoring Services Work

Wake County Public Library has partnered with leading tutoring platforms to provide 24/7 access to certified educators. Here’s a breakdown of how it functions:

1. Platforms Offered

WCPL integrates multiple tutoring services, including:

  • Tutor.com – Offers one-on-one and group sessions in subjects like math, science, and English.
  • Brainfuse – Provides live homework help, skill-building courses, and writing labs.
  • Rosetta Stone – For language learners seeking fluency in Spanish, French, and more.
  • LinkedIn Learning – Access to thousands of video courses on career skills and software training.

2. Who Can Access These Services?

  • K-12 students (public, private, and homeschooled)
  • College students (undergraduate and graduate)
  • Adult learners (career training, GED prep, job readiness)
  • Parents and teachers (for lesson planning and curriculum support)

3. How to Sign Up

  1. Visit the WCPL website (www.wakegov.com/library) and log in with your library card.
  2. Navigate to the "Digital Resources" section.
  3. Select "Tutoring & Homework Help" and choose your preferred platform.
  4. Create an account (if required) and start scheduling sessions.

4. Key Features

Feature Description
Live Tutors Real-time, certified educators available 24/7.
Subject Specialization Tutors in math, science, history, coding, and more.
Test Prep SAT, ACT, AP, and college entrance exam assistance.
Writing Assistance Grammar checks, essay editing, and research help.
Career Resources Resume reviews, interview prep, and LinkedIn optimization.
Multilingual Support Tutors available in Spanish, Mandarin, and other languages.

5. Cost?

Completely free for Wake County residents with a valid library card.

Strategy 1: Schedule Regular Sessions for Consistency

Problem: Many students wait until the last minute to seek help, leading to rushed and ineffective sessions. Solution:

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Online Tutoring

Even the best resources can fail if used incorrectly. Here are 5 mistakes students make—and how to fix them.

Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute

Problem: Students only seek help when exams are days away, making it impossible to catch up. Solution:

  • Start early. If you’re struggling with a topic, book a tutor after the first week of learning it.

Mistake 2: Not Asking Specific Questions

Problem: Vague questions like "I don’t get this" waste tutor time. Solution:

  • Be precise. Instead of "Help me with math," say:
    • "How do I solve systems of equations using substitution?"
    • "Can you explain the difference between mitosis and meiosis?"

Mistake 3: Ignoring Self-Paced Resources

Problem: Some students rely only on tutors and miss out on free practice tools. Solution:

  • Combine tutoring with:
    • Khan Academy videos (for math/science)
    • Grammarly or Hemingway Editor (for writing)
    • Quizlet flashcards (for vocabulary)

Mistake 4: Not Tracking Progress

Problem: Without tracking, students don’t see improvement. Solution:

  • Keep a log of:
    • Tutoring topics covered
    • Test scores before/after sessions
    • Weak areas needing more focus

Mistake 5: Overlooking Career & Soft Skills

Problem: Many students focus only on academics, missing real-world job prep. Solution:

  • Use LinkedIn Learning for:
    • Resume writing
    • Interview skills
    • Industry certifications (Google Analytics, AWS, etc.)
