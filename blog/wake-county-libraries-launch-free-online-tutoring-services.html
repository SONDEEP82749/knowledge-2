To get the most out of the Wake County libraries' online tutoring services, students should consider the following strategies:

  • Prepare ahead of time: Students should come prepared to their online tutoring sessions with any relevant materials, such as textbooks, notes, and assignments. This will help the tutor to understand the student's strengths and weaknesses and provide more effective support.
  • Ask questions and seek feedback: Students should not be afraid to ask questions or seek feedback from their tutors. This will help to ensure that they understand the material and are on track to meet their goals.

  • Stay organized and motivated: With the flexibility of online tutoring comes the risk of procrastination and distraction. Students should stay organized and motivated by setting a schedule and sticking to it, and by rewarding themselves for their progress.
  • Seek additional resources: In addition to the online tutoring services, students can access a range of other resources, including online textbooks, educational videos, and study guides.   • Take breaks and practice self-care: Online learning can be intense and demanding, so it's essential for students to take breaks and practice self-care. This can include taking a walk, meditating, or engaging in a hobby.
  • Monitor progress and adjust the learning plan: Finally, students should regularly monitor their progress and adjust their learning plan as needed. This can involve adjusting the frequency or duration of online tutoring sessions, or seeking additional support from other resources.
