1. Eligibility & Access Requirements

Before diving in, ensure you meet the basic requirements:

  • Residency in Wake County (or a valid Wake County library card).
  • A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access.

💡 Pro Tip: If you don’t have a library card, signing up is quick and free—just visit WCPL’s website and follow the digital registration process.

Common Mistakes & How to Avoid Them

Even the best resources can fail if not used correctly. Here are five mistakes students make—and how to prevent them.

Mistake 1: Waiting Until the Last Minute

Problem: Many students procrastinate and only seek help when deadlines are near, making it harder to grasp concepts.

Solution:

  • Break assignments into smaller chunks and book short, frequent sessions.
  • Use the "Ask a Question" forum for early clarification.

Mistake 2: Not Preparing Before Sessions

Problem: Walking into a tutoring session without clear questions leads to wasted time.

Solution:

  • List 2–3 specific topics you need help with.
  • Bring your notes, textbook, or assignment to the session.

Mistake 3: Ignoring the Writing Lab for Essays

Problem: Students often skip grammar and structure checks, leading to lower grades.

Solution:

  • Always submit drafts to the Writing Lab before finalizing.
  • Use the "Peer Review" feature for additional feedback.

Mistake 4: Not Exploring All Available Resources

Problem: Some users only use live tutoring and miss practice quizzes, video lessons, and forums.

Solution:

  • Check the "Resources" tab for self-paced learning tools.
  • Book a tutor only when needed—don’t rely solely on them.

Mistake 5: Giving Up Too Soon

Problem: If a concept doesn’t click immediately, some students quit trying.

Solution:

  • Revisit topics in multiple sessions—learning is incremental.
  • Ask for alternative explanations if the first approach doesn’t work.
