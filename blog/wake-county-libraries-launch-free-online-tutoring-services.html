  • Choose the right tutor: With a team of experienced and qualified tutors to choose from, it's essential to select a tutor who is a good fit for your needs and learning style. Take the time to read reviews and ask questions before making your selection.
  • Prepare for your sessions: To get the most out of your online tutoring sessions, it's essential to be prepared. This means having all the necessary materials and equipment, such as a computer, internet connection, and headphones, as well as any relevant notes or textbooks.
  • Communicate effectively: Good communication is key to successful online tutoring. Make sure to ask questions, provide feedback, and communicate your needs and concerns clearly to your tutor.
  • Practice regularly: Online tutoring is not a replacement for regular practice and study. Make sure to set aside time each day to practice and reinforce what you have learned during your tutoring sessions.
  • Stay organized: With online tutoring, it's easy to get distracted and lose track of your progress. Stay organized by keeping a schedule, setting reminders, and tracking your progress.
  • Seek help when needed: Don't be afraid to ask for help when you need it. Whether it's during a tutoring session or outside of scheduled sessions, your tutor is there to support you and help you achieve your goals.
  • Provide feedback: Finally, be sure to provide feedback to your tutor and the Wake County libraries' online tutoring service. This will help to improve the quality of the service and ensure that it continues to meet the needs of students like you.
