8 Actionable Strategies to Make the Most of Wake County’s Free Tutoring

Now that you understand how the tutoring works, let’s dive into how to use it effectively to maximize academic growth.

1. Set Clear Goals Before Each Session

Before booking a tutoring session, define what you want to achieve. Ask yourself:

  • Am I struggling with a specific concept (e.g., quadratic equations)?
  • Do I need help with an upcoming test (e.g., SAT Math)?
  • Am I behind on assignments and need catch-up help?

Example: Instead of saying, “I need math help,” specify: “I need help solving systems of equations for my Algebra II test next week.”

2. Prepare Questions in Advance

Tutors can only help as much as you guide them. Before the session:

  • List your biggest challenges (e.g., “I don’t understand how to balance chemical equations”).
  • Bring specific problems you’re stuck on (write them down).
  • Review past notes to refresh your memory.

Common Mistake: Walking into a session with no clear questions leads to wasted time. Tutors can’t read your mind!

3. Use Drop-In Sessions for Quick Doubts

Not every issue requires a full 30-minute session. For small, urgent questions, use:

  • Live chat (available 24/7)
  • Drop-in tutoring (no appointment needed)

Example: A Wake County high school senior needed help with exponent rules before a pop quiz. She used the 24/7 chat to get an instant explanation and aced the quiz.

4. Schedule Regular Sessions for Long-Term Improvement

Consistency is key. If you’re struggling with a subject, book multiple sessions to build confidence.

  • Weekly check-ins for ongoing support
  • Test prep cram sessions before exams
  • Monthly reviews to reinforce learning

Stat: Students who attend at least 4 tutoring sessions see a 40% higher retention rate in difficult subjects (Wake County Library Impact Report, 2024).

5. Leverage Post-Session Resources

After each tutoring session, don’t just forget what you learned. Use the provided:

  • Notes & summaries to review later
  • Practice problems to test your understanding
  • Recommended videos & articles for extra study

Pro Tip: Bookmark the session notes in a Google Doc or OneNote for future reference.

6. Combine Tutoring with Other Free Library Resources

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Using Wake County’s Tutoring Services

While the program is highly effective, some users make preventable mistakes that reduce its impact. Here’s how to avoid them:

❌ Mistake 1: Not Preparing Before the Session

Problem: Walking into a tutoring session with no clear questions leads to wasted time. Solution:

  • List your biggest struggles before the session.
  • Bring specific problems you’re stuck on.
  • Review past notes to refresh your memory.

❌ Mistake 2: Only Using Tutoring for Last-Minute Help

Problem: Cramming for a test the night before doesn’t build long-term understanding. Solution:

  • Schedule regular sessions (weekly or bi-weekly).
  • Use post-session resources to reinforce learning.
  • Apply concepts in homework to solidify knowledge.

❌ Mistake 3: Ignoring Post-Session Materials

Problem: Many students forget to use the notes, practice problems, and links provided. Solution:

  • Save session notes in a Google Doc or OneNote.
  • Complete practice problems within 24 hours of the session.
  • Bookmark recommended resources for future reference.

❌ Mistake 4: Not Communicating Effectively with Tutors

Problem: Vague questions lead to unclear explanations. Solution:

  • Be specific (e.g., “Help me solve this system of equations” instead of “I need math help”).
  • Ask for examples if you’re still confused.
  • Request alternative explanations if the first method doesn’t click.

❌ Mistake 5: Overlooking Free Library Resources

Problem: Some students only use tutoring but miss out on other free tools. Solution:

  • Explore online courses (Coursera, Khan Academy).
  • Borrow e-books & audiobooks for supplementary reading.
  • Use career tools (LinkedIn Learning, Magoosh) for test prep.

❌ Mistake 6: Not Tracking Progress

Problem: Without tracking, it’s hard to see improvement. Solution:

  • Use the parent dashboard (if applicable) to monitor progress.
  • Keep a study journal to note what you learned in each session.
  • Compare grades before and after tutoring to measure success.

❌ Mistake 7: Assuming Tutoring Replaces Self-Study

Problem: Relying only on tutors without practicing on your own leads to short-term gains. Solution:

  • Apply what you learn in homework and practice problems.
  • Review session notes daily.
  • Use additional resources (YouTube, textbooks) for extra practice.
